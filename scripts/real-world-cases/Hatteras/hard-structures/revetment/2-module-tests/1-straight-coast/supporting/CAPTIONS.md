**`straight_coast_revetment.png`.** The simple revetment on a straight coast: 15 domains, a wall on domains 6-8 from year 1 (crest floor 1.2 m above the berm, dashed), -1.0 m/yr background erosion everywhere, CASCADE test waves (Hs 1 m). (a) The shoreline-to-wall gap in each wall domain; the wall is repaired, so once the gap reaches 0 the shoreline is held at the wall. (b) The sand that takes, added through CASCADE's nourishment path. (c) Mean dune crest in the middle wall domain. (d) Cumulative overwash, mean of the wall domains. (e) Shoreline position relative to the no-wall run in the middle wall domain, a neighbour and two domains further away. (f) Shoreline change over the run, seaward positive.
