# Captions — figures

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`brie_sand_the_idea.png`.** **Tests:** nothing; the idea behind BRIE's sand error, without the model. **How to read:** two neighbouring sections of coast and the boundary between them. Every handover of sand across the boundary is written down twice, once by each section. Left: both write the same amount, so the island's total is unchanged. Middle: BRIE lets each section work out the amount with its own rate; when the two rates are the same the books still agree. Right: when the rates differ, one side books more than the other, and the difference is sand made or lost. The numbers are illustrative. **Shows:** sand can only be made or lost if the two sides of a boundary use different rates, which is what BRIE does at a bend (../3-one-year/).
