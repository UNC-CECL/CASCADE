# Captions — figures

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`brie_sand_total_over_time.png`.** **Tests:** whether BRIE's alongshore step keeps the island's sand constant when nothing adds or removes any, using an unmodified `brie.Brie` with its barrier and inlet models off, so `update()` moves the shoreline by the alongshore step alone. Option A waves, 30 domains of 500 m on BRIE's periodic coast, 50 one-year steps. **How to read:** (a) and (b) the shoreline at the start (dashed), after 50 years of BRIE as written, and after the same years with each boundary's rate shared by the two domains it joins (green). (c) the island's summed shoreline against its start: zero means sand is conserved. The volume uses BRIE's active depth (19.8 m) and 500 m domains. **Shows:** on a straight coast nothing is made or lost (0.000 m); a gentle 100 m bump loses -10.6 m in 50 years; a sharp 100 m step loses -117.9 m, 39% of the sand in it. With one rate per boundary every case conserves to round-off, so the loss comes from the two domains at a bend using different rates for the boundary between them, not from the waves or the equation.
