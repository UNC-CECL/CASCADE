bridged between control points with a straight line, before the hybrid in
`1-produce/island_offset_hybrid.py` replaced it.

**Dated 2026-09-14**, from git: the last commit that touched the folder
(`1aa3b3aa`, "Every rule but one reads clean"). That is the best available
evidence of when work on it stopped, not necessarily the day a decision was
taken.

**Renamed from `old-linear-bridge/` on 2026-09-22.** Rule 4 of
`ORGANIZATION.md` asks for `superseded_<date>/` with a note, and says in as
many words: not `old_`, not `_ARCHIVE_`, not `_backup`, not a bare `old/`. A
date says when the decision was taken; the note says what it was. `old-` said
neither, and this was the only retirement folder under `scripts/` still
carrying that form outside an already-dated parent.

Nothing here is maintained or expected to run.
