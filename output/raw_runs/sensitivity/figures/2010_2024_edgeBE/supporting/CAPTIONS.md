**`06_best_setting_optionA.png`.** Option A, the best wave setting found by the sweep and the matrix baseline it moves around (the pink line in 02-05). Modelled (CASCADE, orange) and observed (CoastSat, blue) shoreline change rate along Hatteras Island, 2010–2024, GIS domain 1 (Cape Point) to 90 (Pea Island); positive is seaward (accretion). Observed rate is the linear regression rate (LRR) per transect, smoothed with a 7-domain LOWESS north of domain 10; south of it LOWESS is not applied and the line is the unsmoothed domain mean (dashed), with the individual transects shown as dots. Grey bands: communities. Hatched: shoal zones. Dash-dot: piers. Dotted: Buxton groin. Wave climate: Hs 2.0 m, Tp 7.5 s, asymmetry 0.6, high-angle fraction 0.5. Background erosion on.
