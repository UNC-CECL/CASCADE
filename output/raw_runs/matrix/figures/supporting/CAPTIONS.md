# Captions — figures

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`management_ladder_overlay_net_change_edgeBE.png`.** The management ladder overlaid, end rates solved on CoastSat (edgeBE): every run of a window in one panel, natural to full management, in the ladder figures' colours (orange natural, sky blue + roads, green + beach and dune management, vermillion + fills; the last rung heaviest) under the black target. The modelled net change (final minus initial state), smoothed as the target is. RMSE and bias over the interior GIS 2–89 in the legend; seaward positive. Each rung uses the window's full-management end rates. Targets: (a) 1996–2009: the CoastSat net-change target (black; the mean shoreline over 2009-01-01 to 2009-12-31 minus the mean over 1996-01-01 to 1996-12-31, the island offset's window; 7-domain LOWESS, GIS 1–10 raw); (b) 2009–2025: the CoastSat net-change target (black; the mean shoreline over 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31 minus the mean over 2009-01-01 to 2009-12-31, the island offset's window; 7-domain LOWESS, GIS 1–10 raw); (c) 1996–2025: the CoastSat net-change target (black; the mean shoreline over 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31 minus the mean over 1996-01-01 to 1996-12-31, the island offset's window; 7-domain LOWESS, GIS 1–10 raw). Hatched gold: the Avon (GIS 26–38) and Wimble (GIS 61–74) shoal zones. A value beyond the axis is marked with a triangle at the edge.
