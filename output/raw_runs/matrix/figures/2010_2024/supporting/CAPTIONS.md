# Captions — 2010_2024

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`management_ladder_rate_edgeBE_2010_2024.png`.** The 2010–2024 option A matrix runs, end rates solved on CoastSat (edgeBE), in order of increasing management. Each panel adds one layer to the panel above and draws every run up to that point: the modelled OLS shoreline-change rate of each rung in its own blue (lighter = less managed; the newest rung heaviest, and each rung keeps its colour down the figure), over the CoastSat LRR scoring target (black; 7-domain LOWESS, raw domain means GIS 1–10). RMSE and bias of the newest rung over the interior GIS 2–89 (GIS 1 at Cape Point, 90 at Pea Island); seaward positive. Rungs the window lacks are skipped: 1996–2010 has no nourishment fills. The historical-relocation runs are not on the ladder (relocation moves the road, not the shoreline). Runs: (a) HAT_2010_2024_edgeBE_offsetmetres_noroad_nobdm_nogroin; (b) HAT_2010_2024_edgeBE_offsetmetres_road_nobdm_nogroin; (c) HAT_2010_2024_edgeBE_offsetmetres_road_bdm_nonourish_nogroin; (d) HAT_2010_2024_edgeBE_offsetmetres_road_bdm_nourish_nogroin.
