# Captions — 2009_2025

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`management_ladder_rate_edgeBE_2009_2025.png`.** The 2009–2025 matrix runs, end rates solved on CoastSat (edgeBE), in order of increasing management. Each panel adds one layer to the panel above and draws every run up to that point: the modelled OLS shoreline-change rate of each rung in its own colour (Okabe-Ito; the newest rung heaviest, and each rung keeps its colour down the figure), under the CoastSat LRR scoring target (black; 7-domain LOWESS, raw domain means GIS 1–10). RMSE and bias of the newest rung over the interior GIS 2–89 (GIS 1 at Cape Point, 90 at Pea Island); seaward positive. Every model line is smoothed as the observation is (7-domain LOWESS, GIS 1–10 raw). A rung that adds nothing in this window is skipped. Hatched gold: the Avon (GIS 26–38) and Wimble (GIS 61–74) shoal zones. A value beyond the axis (GIS 1, observed and modelled) is marked with a triangle at the edge. The historical-relocation runs are not on the ladder (relocation moves the road, not the shoreline). Runs: (a) HAT_2009_2025_edgeBE_offsetmetres_noroad_nobdm_nogroin; (b) HAT_2009_2025_edgeBE_offsetmetres_road_nobdm_nogroin; (c) HAT_2009_2025_edgeBE_offsetmetres_road_bdm_nonourish_nogroin; (d) HAT_2009_2025_edgeBE_offsetmetres_road_bdm_nourish_nogroin.
