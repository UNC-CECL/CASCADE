# storms-and-overwash

What the model's storm series contains, and whether overwash happens where and when it should.

Including the dropped storms raises the 1996–2010 observed-overwash hit rate from about 15% to 70–80% but adds retreat under the 72 h calibration. | record; led to the adoption (storms `v3_trim24`, every event kept) |
| [`2026-09-28-storm-length-selection`](2026-09-28-storm-length-selection/NOTE.md) | Which storm duration rule (drop events over 72 h, or keep all and trim to 24–168 h, or full) matches the observed overwash best? | Stage 1: keeping the dropped storms raises the 1996–2010 Peirce skill from −0.07 to about 0.35 but lowers it in 2010–2024 (0.37 → 0.03–0.14, more false alarms). Trim length barely changes overwash, but RMSE rises with it; trim24 is the best compromise. Every series overwashes far more than the imagery shows. | record; stage 2 not run (the excess overwash came first) |
| [`2026-09-28-excess-overwash-diagnosis`](2026-09-28-excess-overwash-diagnosis/NOTE.md) | Why does the model overwash far more than the imagery shows? | The dunes are held at about 3 m MHW when real ones are about 5 m. `Dmaxel` was never set for Hatteras (it is Barrier3D's 3.4 m NAVD88 default, so dunes shrink to about 3.04 m MHW every year), and the NC-12 rebuild lowers the whole dune field to the 3.0 m design height. By 2010 the model's crests are 2.7–3.4 m below the lidar. Clipped dune windows are not the cause. | record; fixed by the adoption |
| [`2026-09-28-dune-ceiling-and-rebuild`](2026-09-28-dune-ceiling-and-rebuild/NOTE.md) | Does a Hatteras dune ceiling (Dmaxel 3.4/5.5/7.5/9 m NAVD88) or a rebuild that never lowers dunes fix the excess overwash? | Dmaxel is the control and 5.5 m matches the lidar (2010 crest within 0.2 m). With trim24 storms the 1996–2010 Peirce skill goes −0.07 → 0.60 (timing r 0.97). 2010–2024 false alarms fall 44% → 8% and RMSE improves 2.25 → 1.91, but the hit rate drops to 0.23: one ceiling erases real low spots. The rebuild rule barely matters. | record; superseded by the per-cell ceiling |
| [`2026-09-28-dune-ceiling-per-domain`](2026-09-28-dune-ceiling-per-domain/NOTE.md) | Does a ceiling from each domain's (or each cell's) own starting dunes keep the low spots? | Yes. The crest pattern vs lidar goes from r 0.21 to about 0.55, and Irene's low-dune domains overwash in 26 of 30 against 25 observed. Per-cell is nearly as skilful as uniform 5.5 m in 1996–2010 (PSS 0.57 vs 0.60) with the best spatial r (0.63), and doubles the 2010–2024 hit rate. What remains is Irene overtopping 4.3 m+ dunes in reality (47 of 60 observed, 10 modelled): storm height, not dunes. | **current**: per-cell ceilings ADOPTED 2026-09-28 (Barrier3D `hatteras/adopted`) |
| [`2026-09-28-storm-height`](2026-09-28-storm-height/NOTE.md) | Are the storm water levels (Duck gauge, run-up slope 0.06) too low for Hatteras? | No. The ocean-side Cape Hatteras pier matches Duck (median +0.05 m over 5 storms) and the measured foreshore slope is 0.067. Irene's high water was sound-side (Oregon Inlet 2.02 m vs Duck 0.56 m), which Barrier3D does not model. Raising storms 0.25–0.5 m barely helps; slope 0.10 recovers Irene only by overwashing everywhere (false alarms 56–69%). | record |
| [`2026-09-28-trim-length-adopted`](2026-09-28-trim-length-adopted/NOTE.md) | On the adopted setup (overwash fixes + per-cell ceiling), does the storm trim length (12/24/48/72 h/full) still matter? | Not for where or when overwash happens (PSS, timing and Irene unchanged). Volume grows 6–8× and the shoreline bias moves landward with length. 12 h and 24 h are tied (1996 favours 24, 2010 favours 12); longer trims are worse. Confirms trim24. | **current**: trim24 adopted |
