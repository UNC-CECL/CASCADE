# Captions — calendar_year_shorelines

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`calendar_year_shorelines_1996_2009_2025.png`.** CoastSat mean shorelines for calendar years 1996 (red), 2009 (amber) and 2025 (blue, drawn on top): each transect's mean over 1 Jan to 31 Dec of that year, min 5 positions. Under the advisor's 2026-10-12 proposal, 1996 starts calibration (storms 1997-2009), 2009 ends calibration and starts the test (storms 2010-2025), and 2025 ends the test. Six consecutive sections of Hatteras, south to north from left to right, alongshore up the page. Each line is the distance from the shared offshore datum along the 100 m model transects, averaged per 500 m domain, ocean on the right, computed with the island-offset build's own intersection. Median satellite positions per transect: 1996 13, 2009 9, 2025 37. Change per domain, seaward +, island medians 1996→2009 -2.5 m, 2009→2025 +4.0 m. No smoothing. Village spans shaded grey; shoal zones (Avon Shoals GIS 26-38, Wimble Shoals GIS 61-74) hatched; piers dash-dot blue, Buxton groin dotted red. Per-domain numbers in calendar_year_shorelines_1996_2009_2025.csv.
