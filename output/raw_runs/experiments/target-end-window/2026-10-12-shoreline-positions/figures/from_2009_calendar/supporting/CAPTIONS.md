# Captions — from_2009_calendar

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`target_shoreline_positions_1996_from_2009_calendar.png`.** Sensitivity of the 1996 target shoreline (start of calibration) to the averaging window. Each line is the CoastSat mean shoreline over one window, per 500 m GIS domain, south to north; the same five windows are applied to each target year (1996, 2009, 2025), each with the same colour in all three figures. The calendar-year 1996 mean is the advisor's 2026-10-12 proposal. Distance seaward of the calendar-2009 mean shoreline (1 Jan-31 Dec 2009), the start line of the test period, the same fixed line in all three panels, so the 2009 figure's proposed line is zero by construction and the 2025 figure shows the 2009→2025 change the test is scored against; subtracting one fixed line shifts every window alike, so the spread between windows is the same as in the other frames. Every line is intersected with the 100 m model transects by the island-offset build's own intersection (step1_line_to_raw_offsets.intersect) and averaged per 500 m GIS domain. Median satellite positions per transect: Calendar year 1996 ± 6 months 21; Three calendar years 28; 1 Jan 1996 ± 1 yr 22; 1 Jan 1996 ± 6 months 13; Calendar year 1996 13; minimum 5 per transect. Solid lines: 7-domain LOWESS, GIS 1-10 raw (the smoothing of the 2026-10-07 figures and of the calibration and test targets); the unsmoothed domain means are in the raw CSV. Shaded: communities; hatched: shoal zones; dash-dot: piers; dotted: Buxton groin. Per-domain values in target_shoreline_positions_from_2009_calendar.csv. The y-axis is the same in the 1996, 2009 and 2025 figures (-100 to 100 m).
