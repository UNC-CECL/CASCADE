# Captions — from_1996_calendar

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`target_shoreline_positions_1996_from_1996_calendar.png`.** Sensitivity of the 1996 target shoreline (start of calibration) to the averaging window. Each line is the CoastSat mean shoreline over one window, per 500 m GIS domain, south to north; the same five windows are applied to each target year (1996, 2009, 2025), each with the same colour in all three figures. The calendar-year 1996 mean is the advisor's 2026-10-12 proposal. Distance seaward of the calendar-1996 mean shoreline (1 Jan-31 Dec 1996), the same fixed line in all three panels, so panel (a)'s proposed line is zero by construction: every line is intersected with the 100 m model transects by the island-offset build's own intersection (step1_line_to_raw_offsets.intersect) and averaged per 500 m GIS domain. Median satellite positions per transect: Calendar year 1996 ± 6 months 21; Three calendar years 28; 1 Jan 1996 ± 1 yr 22; 1 Jan 1996 ± 6 months 13; Calendar year 1996 13; minimum 5 per transect. Solid lines: 7-domain LOWESS, GIS 1-10 raw (the smoothing of the 2026-10-07 figures and of the calibration and test targets); the unsmoothed domain means are in the raw CSV. Shaded: communities; hatched: shoal zones; dash-dot: piers; dotted: Buxton groin. Per-domain values in target_shoreline_positions_from_1996_calendar.csv. The y-axis is the same in the 1996, 2009 and 2025 figures (-100 to 100 m).
