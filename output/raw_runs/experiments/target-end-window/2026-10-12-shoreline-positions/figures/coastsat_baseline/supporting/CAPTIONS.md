# Captions — coastsat_baseline

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`target_shoreline_positions_1996_coastsat_baseline.png`.** Sensitivity of the 1996 target shoreline (start of calibration) to the averaging window. Each line is the CoastSat mean shoreline over one window, per 500 m GIS domain, south to north; the same five windows are applied to each target year (1996, 2009, 2025), each with the same colour in all three figures. The calendar-year 1996 mean is the advisor's 2026-10-12 proposal. CoastSat's own cross-shore position: the distance along each CoastSat transect from its origin, averaged per 500 m GIS domain over the transects that every window of that year includes. The CoastSat baseline is built from site segments and steps between some domains; the steps are the baseline's, not the beach's, and sit in every line alike. Median satellite positions per transect: Calendar year 1996 ± 6 months 21; Three calendar years 28; 1 Jan 1996 ± 1 yr 22; 1 Jan 1996 ± 6 months 13; Calendar year 1996 13; minimum 5 per transect. Solid lines: 7-domain LOWESS, GIS 1-10 raw (the smoothing of the 2026-10-07 figures and of the calibration and test targets); the unsmoothed domain means are in the raw CSV. Shaded: communities; hatched: shoal zones; dash-dot: piers; dotted: Buxton groin. Per-domain values in target_shoreline_positions_coastsat_baseline.csv. The y-axis is the same in the 1996, 2009 and 2025 figures (0 to 120 m).
