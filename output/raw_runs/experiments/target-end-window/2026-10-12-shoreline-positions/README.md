# 2026-10-12-shoreline-positions

**Question (advisor, 2026-10-12):** what do the mean shorelines of her proposal look like as positions, not net change? Under the proposal each period starts and ends on a calendar-year CoastSat mean: calibration 1996 -> 2009 (storms 1997-2009), test 2009 -> 2025 (storms 2010-2025).

**Producer:** `end_line_positions.py` -> `figures/calendar_year_shorelines/calendar_year_shorelines_1996_2009_2025.png` (+ `supporting/` PDF, CAPTIONS.md, per-domain CSV). Three lines: calendar 1996, 2009 and 2025, each the distance from the offshore datum per GIS domain by the island-offset build's own intersection, in the six-section layout of `mean_shoreline/compared/`. No runs, no smoothing. The calendar-1996 mean (`mean_shoreline/1996-01-01_1996-12-31/`, min 5 positions) was built for this figure.

**Result:** median positions per transect 1996 13, 2009 9, 2025 37. Island medians: 1996 -> 2009 -2.5 m (-69 to +51 m), 2009 -> 2025 +4.0 m (-38 to +134 m, GIS 1), seaward +.

**Second figure, a first reading of the advisor's request (superseded by the third and fourth):** `oct07_end_lines_positions.py` -> `figures/end_lines_2025/end_lines_2025_positions.png`: test end lines of the 10-07 test figure (1 Jul 2024 ±1 yr, 1 Jan 2025 ±1 yr, 1 Jan 2025 ±6 months, plus calendar 2025), with its colours, as positions, styled like the three-line figure. The DEM-date window (2025-02-17 to 2026-02-17) is left out at Hannah's request. The four lines overlap at island scale: median spread 5.9 m per domain, at most 35.2 m (GIS 1, Cape Point). A first version drew the 10-07 calibration figure's 2009 lines instead; Hannah corrected it the same day.

**Style (Hannah, 2026-10-12):** both figures use thin solid lines (lw 1.0), the end line drawn last on top; village spans shaded grey; shoal zones hatched as in the net-change figures (Avon Shoals GIS 26-38, Wimble Shoals GIS 61-74, from `HATTERAS_ANNOTATIONS`); piers (dash-dot blue) and the Buxton groin (dotted red) as horizontal lines at their GIS position. The helpers live in `end_line_positions.py`; `oct07_end_lines_positions.py` imports them.

**Third and fourth figures, what the advisor actually meant (clarified 2026-10-12, 5:35 PM):** "the different ways of calculating the 1996 / 2009 / 2025 all together, alongshore, averaged for each domain, like the plot from October 7 but with shoreline position, not net change." `target_shoreline_positions.py` draws five averaging windows for each target year (the methods below) in the 10-07 style, one figure per year (`figures/<frame>/target_shoreline_positions_<year>_<frame>.png`, six in all), in two position frames (Hannah chose both):

- `coastsat_baseline`: CoastSat's own position, distance from each CoastSat transect's origin, averaged per domain over the transects every window of that year includes. The CoastSat baseline steps between site segments; the steps are in every line alike.
- `from_1996_calendar`: distance seaward of the calendar-1996 mean shoreline, the same fixed line in all three panels (Hannah: "in relation to the 1996 calendar year shoreline, not the dune line"), on the model transects by the offset build's intersection.

**Methods (Hannah, 2026-10-12):** the same five windows applied to each target year Y, one colour each in every figure (palette A 'Coastal', chosen from three previews; no red or blue, which mean calibration/test; all lines one width): Y ±6 months (1 Jul Y-1 to 30 Jun Y+1) teal #2a9d8f; three calendar years (Y-1 to Y+1) mauve #8e6c8a; 1 Jan Y ±1 yr (Y-1 to Y) gold #e9b949; 1 Jan Y ±6 months (1 Jul Y-1 to 1 Jul Y) terracotta #e07a5f; calendar year Y (the proposal) near-black #1d1d1d, drawn last. All windows at min 5 positions per transect; seven were built for this (1995-07-01_1997-06-30, 1995-01-01_1997-12-31, 1995-01-01_1997-01-01, 1995-07-01_1996-07-01, 2008-07-01_2010-06-30, 2008-01-01_2010-12-31, 2008-07-01_2009-07-01). Earlier sets (every window considered, then the 10-07 lines, DEM-date windows) were replaced the same day.


**Figure folders:** `figures/coastsat_baseline/` and `figures/from_1996_calendar/` (the advisor's figures, three years each), `figures/calendar_year_shorelines/` (the proposal's three calendar-year lines), `figures/end_lines_2025/` (first reading of the request).
