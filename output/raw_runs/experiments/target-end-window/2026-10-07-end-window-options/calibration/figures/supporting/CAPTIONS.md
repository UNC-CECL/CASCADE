**`end_lines_vs_model_calibration_zeroBE.png`.** Modelled and observed 1996-2009 net shoreline change per GIS domain, calibration period, zeroBE, no groin. Observed: four end lines, each minus the CoastSat mean over 1995-10-12 to 1997-10-12. Old target: 17 Aug 2009 (DEM date) ±1 yr: end mean 2008-08-17 to 2010-08-17. Current target: 1 Jul 2008 ±1 yr: end mean 2007-07-01 to 2009-06-30. 1 Jan 2009 (model end) ±1 yr: end mean 2008-01-01 to 2010-01-01. Calendar year 2009 (min 5 positions): end mean 2009-01-01 to 2009-12-31. Dashed: the zeroBE, no groin run, 1 Jan 1996 to 1 Jan 2009. All at 7-domain LOWESS, GIS 1-10 raw. Shaded bands: communities; dashed lines: village centres; hatched: shoal zones; dash-dot: piers; dotted: Buxton groin. Seaward positive.
