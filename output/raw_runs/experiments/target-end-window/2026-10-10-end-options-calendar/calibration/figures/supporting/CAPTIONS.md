# Captions — figures

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`end_options_calibration_zeroBE.png`.** Modelled and observed net shoreline change per GIS domain, Calibration 1996-2009, zeroBE, no groin. Observed: five end windows, each minus the CoastSat mean over 1995-10-12 to 1997-10-12; red (drawn on top) is the selected end (the last modelled year, 2008, ±6 months). Selected: 2008 ±6 months: end mean 2007-07-01 to 2009-06-30. Last modelled year only (2008): end mean 2008-01-01 to 2008-12-31. Last two modelled years (2007-2008): end mean 2007-01-01 to 2008-12-31. 2008 ±1 yr, 3-yr window (2007-2009): end mean 2007-01-01 to 2009-12-31. Model end state (1 Jan 2009) ±1 yr: end mean 2008-01-01 to 2010-01-01. CoastSat ends 2026-01-13, so windows reaching past it hold only what precedes it. Dashed: the zeroBE, no groin run, 1 Jan 1996 to 1 Jan 2009; RMSE and bias (m, interior GIS 2-89) in the legend. All at 7-domain LOWESS, GIS 1-10 raw. Shaded bands: communities; dashed lines: village centres; hatched: shoal zones; dash-dot: piers; dotted: Buxton groin. Seaward positive.
