# Captions — figures

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`start_offsets_1yr_vs_6mo.png`.** The two candidate start lines for each period, as island offsets the model reads. Top: the unpadded offset built from the CoastSat mean shoreline over ±1 yr of the DEM date (v2, red; the current input) and over ±6 months (v3, blue), each zeroed on its own most seaward domain; dates in the legends. At this scale the two coincide. Bottom: ±6 months minus ±1 yr in that frame, shared axis; positive is a larger offset in the ±6-month build. 1996: mean +1.3 m, range -3.7 to +7.6 m; 2009: mean +3.0 m, range -3.3 to +9.9 m. The ±6-month lines were built with a minimum of 5 positions per transect, against 10 for ±1 yr.

**`model_1yr_vs_6mo.png`.** Net shoreline change per GIS domain from the two candidate start lines. Red: the ±1-yr CoastSat mean around the DEM date (offset v2, the production matrix runs); blue: the ±6-month mean (offset v3, the 6mo runs). Solid: the observed target, the end window (calibration 1 Jul 2007 to 30 Jun 2009, test 17 Feb 2025 to 17 Feb 2026) minus the mean over that start window; dashed: the run started from that line. Scores (m, interior GIS 2-89) in each panel. All at 7-domain LOWESS. Seaward positive. Beyond ±100 m, off the axis and marked with a triangle at the edge: Test 2009-2025 ±1 yr start target +158 m at GIS 1; Test 2009-2025 ±6 month start target +158 m at GIS 1; Test 2009-2025 edgeBE, no groin ±1 yr start run +158 m at GIS 1; Test 2009-2025 edgeBE, no groin ±6 month start run +161 m at GIS 1.
