# Captions — 3-both_windows

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`fit_edgeBE_and_zeroBE_1996_2015_and_2010_2026_lowess5_and_lowess7.png`.** Observed and modelled shoreline change rate by GIS domain (1 at Cape Point, 90 at Pea Island) on the calibration window (a, 1996–2015) and the test window (b, 2010–2026), each LOWESS-smoothed alongshore at 3.5 km (7 domains, dark) and 2.5 km (5 domains, light), both sides the same way, with raw domain means kept over GIS 1–10. Blue is the CoastSat linear regression rate over the calendar window, with the 95% band on the 7-domain curve (ordinary least-squares, too narrow for a serially correlated record). Green is edgeBE, which carries end rates solved against the observed window (1996–2015: GIS 1 +3.39, GIS 90 +37.60; 2010–2026: GIS 1 +172.89, GIS 90 +24.08 m/yr); grey is zeroBE, no source or sink, drawn underneath and visible only where it leaves edgeBE near the ends. Both model periods match their observed windows; the test-window run is forced through 2025 with the Duck water levels and WIS waves extended past the 1984–2024 records. Its edgeBE GIS 1 end is 22 times the adopted value, so that model's agreement at Cape Point is forced. All runs are full management (fills in the window included), no groin, no relocation. Grey bands are the villages, amber tints the offshore shoals; the solid hairline is the Buxton groin, the dotted ones the Avon and Rodanthe piers. The y axis is clipped at ±5 m/yr; a value beyond it is marked by a triangle at the edge, with the largest labelled.

**`misfit_edgeBE_and_zeroBE_1996_2015_and_2010_2026_lowess5_and_lowess7.png`.** Model minus observed shoreline change rate by GIS domain, both sides LOWESS-smoothed at the same width: below zero the model erodes more (or accretes less) than the shoreline did. Colour is the width, light green 2.5 km (5 domains) and dark green 3.5 km (7 domains); line style is the model, solid edgeBE (ends solved against the window) and dotted zeroBE (no source or sink). Away from the ends the two models coincide and the width moves a line by tenths of a metre a year. The y axis is clipped at ±4 m/yr; a value beyond it is marked by a triangle at the edge, with the largest labelled.
