Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`shoreline_position_change_with_buffers.png`.** Shoreline position change along Hatteras Island, 2009–2025 (test period), from GIS domain 1 (Cape Point) to 90 (Pea Island); positive is seaward. Modelled (CASCADE, orange): the shoreline at the end of the 16-year run (1 Jan 2025) minus the start, with source/sink at the end domains only (GIS 1 and 90), no groin and road maintenance, beach and dune management, nourishment; thick: smoothed with a 7-domain LOWESS north of domain 10 (unsmoothed south of it), as in the skill scores; thin: each domain's unsmoothed value. The start shoreline is the CoastSat mean over 2008-08-17 to 2010-08-17. Observed (CoastSat, blue): per transect, the mean shoreline position over 2025-02-17 to 2026-02-17 (centred on 2025-08-17) minus the mean over 2008-08-17 to 2010-08-17 (centred on 2009-08-17). CoastSat ends on 2026-01-13, so that mean covers 2025-02-17 to 2026-01-13. Thin line: the mean of each 500 m domain's transects. Thick line: the transect values smoothed with a 7-domain LOWESS north of domain 10; south of it the individual transects are shown as dots. Blue shading: the smoothed observed change against zero. Shaded bands: communities; dashed lines: village centres; hatched: shoal zones; dash-dot: piers; dotted: Buxton groin. The y axis is fixed at ±100 m on every run so figures compare. Beyond ±100 m, off the axis and marked with a triangle at the edge: CASCADE (smoothed) +209 m at GIS 1; CASCADE (unsmoothed) +209 m at GIS 1; CoastSat domain mean +158 m at GIS 1; CoastSat LOWESS +118 m at GIS 1. 12 transects at GIS 1–2 are off the axis too (up to +210 m), marked at the edge. Wave climate: Hs 2.0 m, Tp 7.5 s, asym 0.6, high-angle 0.5. Run HAT_2009_2025_edgeBE_offsetmetres_road_bdm_nourish_nogroin.
