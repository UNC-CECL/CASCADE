# Captions — figures

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`duneline_offset_vs_duneline_change_full_management.png`.** The model started from the DUNE-LINE island offset, against the dune line's own change. Net change per domain, seaward positive: observed (black) is the mean change between the digitised dune lines that bound each period (1997-10 to 2009-05, 11.6 yr, for 1996-2010; 2009-05 to 2023-07, 14.1 yr, for 2010-2024), LOWESS over 7 domains (the southern 10 raw); the model (green) is unsmoothed, its endpoint change over the 14 calendar years. The interval mismatch is not corrected. No source/sink correction at the ends. Amber: Avon and Wimble Shoals; solid line: Buxton groin; dotted lines: Avon and Rodanthe piers; grey strip: villages; bars above a panel: the nourishments the model is given in that period, footprint and year. (a) full management 1996-2010, (b) full management 2010-2024; waves Hs 1 m, Tp 8 s, asymmetry 0.8, high-angle 0.45 (the 09-25 setting, tuned with the dune-line offset; superseded by option A on 09-27); island offset in metres (duneline/v1, shoreline/v1); no source/sink correction at the ends (zeroBE); relocations and groins off. The natural runs are in tables/all_runs.csv. Redrawn 2026-09-28 in the form of ../2026-09-28-metres-offset-duneline-vs-shoreline-waves-option-a/. The three comparison figures share one y-axis; the difference figure has its own, symmetric about zero.
