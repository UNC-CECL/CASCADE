# Captions — figures

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`duneline_offset_vs_duneline_change_1996_2010.png`.** The model started from the DUNE-LINE island offset, against the dune line's own change. Net change per domain, seaward positive: observed (black) is the mean change between the digitised dune lines that bound each period (1997-10 to 2009-05, 11.6 yr, for 1996-2010; 2009-05 to 2023-07, 14.1 yr, for 2010-2024), LOWESS over 7 domains (the southern 10 raw); the model (green) is unsmoothed, its endpoint change over the 14 calendar years. The interval mismatch is not corrected. Amber: Avon and Wimble Shoals; solid line: Buxton groin; dotted lines: Avon and Rodanthe piers; grey strip: villages; bars above a panel: the nourishments the model is given in that period, footprint and year. (a) full management 1996-2010, (b) full management 2010-2024; waves Hs 2.5 m, Tp 8 s, asymmetry 0.7, high-angle 0.1 (the ÷10-era defaults, before 2026-09-24); island offset ÷10: offset_mode asrun (offset / 10) on superseded_20260924_pre-metres/v1 of each source, as before the metres fix (2010 shoreline: its v1, the only build, which differs from a pre-metres one only in the buffers); no source/sink correction at the ends (zeroBE); relocations and groins off. The natural runs are in tables/all_runs.csv. A REBUILD (2026-09-28), not a record: the 09-25 design on the ÷10 offset; compare ../2026-09-25-metres-offset-duneline-vs-shoreline-waves-hs1-tp8/ (metres, 09-25 waves).
