**`buxton_2017_reported_vs_shifted_north_net_change_2009_2025.png`.** Experiment: the 2017 Buxton fill moved 2 domains north. Shoreline position change along Hatteras Island, 2009–2025 (test period), from GIS domain 1 (Cape Point) to 90 (Pea Island); positive is seaward. Both model runs are the 16-year test run (1 Jan 2025 minus the start) with per-domain source/sink (set 1), blocking groin, road maintenance, beach and dune management and nourishment, relocations off. Solid orange: the 2017 fill on GIS 8–18; thick smoothed with a 7-domain LOWESS north of domain 10 (unsmoothed south of it and at domain 90), thin each domain's unsmoothed value. Dashed orange: the same run with the 2017 fill on its reported footprint, GIS 6–16, smoothed the same way; it is the matrix run exactly. The fill's volume (2.6 M cy) and width are unchanged, so the sand per metre is the same; the 2022 Buxton fill stays on GIS 6–16 in both. Bars near the top mark the two 2017 footprints. Legend RMSE is the interior score, GIS 2–89, both sides smoothed; bias is -18.2 m (reported) and -18.2 m (shifted), r 0.36 and 0.43. Outside GIS 3–20 the runs are identical and the solid line hides the dashed one. Observed (CoastSat, blue): per transect, the mean shoreline position over 2025-02-17 to 2026-02-17 (centred on 2025-08-17) minus the mean over 2008-08-17 to 2010-08-17 (centred on 2009-08-17). CoastSat ends on 2026-01-13, so that mean covers 2025-02-17 to 2026-01-13. Thin line: the mean of each 500 m domain's transects. Thick line: the transect values smoothed with a 7-domain LOWESS north of domain 10; south of it the individual transects are shown as dots. Blue shading: the smoothed observed change against zero. Shaded bands: communities; dashed lines: village centres; hatched: shoal zones; dash-dot: piers; dotted: Buxton groin. The y axis is fixed at ±100 m as on every run figure. Beyond ±100 m, off the axis and marked with a triangle at the edge: CoastSat domain mean +158 m at GIS 1; CoastSat LOWESS +118 m at GIS 1. 12 transects at GIS 1–2 are off the axis too (up to +210 m), marked at the edge. Wave climate: Hs 2.0 m, Tp 7.5 s, asym 0.6, high-angle 0.5.
