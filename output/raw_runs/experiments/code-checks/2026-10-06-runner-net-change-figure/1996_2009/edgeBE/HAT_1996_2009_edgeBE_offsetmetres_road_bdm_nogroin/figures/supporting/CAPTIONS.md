# Captions — figures

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`shoreline_position_change_with_buffers.png`.** Shoreline position change along Hatteras Island, 1996–2009 (calibration period), from GIS domain 1 (Cape Point) to 90 (Pea Island); positive is seaward. Modelled (CASCADE, orange): the shoreline at the end of the 13-year run (1 Jan 2009) minus the start, with source/sink at the end domains only (GIS 1 and 90), no groin and road maintenance, beach and dune management; thick: smoothed with a 7-domain LOWESS north of domain 10 (unsmoothed south of it and at domain 90, the end value the source/sink solve matches); thin: each domain's unsmoothed value. The start shoreline is the CoastSat mean over 1995-10-12 to 1997-10-12. Observed (CoastSat, blue): per transect, the mean shoreline position over 2008-08-17 to 2010-08-17 (centred on 2009-08-17) minus the mean over 1995-10-12 to 1997-10-12 (centred on 1996-10-12). Thin line: the mean of each 500 m domain's transects. Thick line: the transect values smoothed with a 7-domain LOWESS north of domain 10; south of it the individual transects are shown as dots. Blue shading: the smoothed observed change against zero. Shaded bands: communities; dashed lines: village centres; hatched: shoal zones; dash-dot: piers; dotted: Buxton groin. The y axis is fixed at ±100 m on every run so figures compare. Wave climate: Hs 2.0 m, Tp 7.5 s, asym 0.6, high-angle 0.5. Run HAT_1996_2009_edgeBE_offsetmetres_road_bdm_nogroin.
