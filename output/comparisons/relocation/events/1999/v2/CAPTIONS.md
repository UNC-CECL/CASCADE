# Captions -- 1999 relocation across hindcast windows

**setback_trajectories_1999_zeroBE.png** -- NC-12 setback behind the dune line at each domain the 1999 relocation moved (GIS 9-14), under the zeroBE source/sink preset, for the two hindcast windows that contain the event. Red is the 1984 start, blue the 1996 start; solid is the emergent arm (the roadway module deciding alone), dashed the prescribed arm (the measured 1999 displacement applied). The dotted rule is the event year, the cross the surveyed 2004 road position (RoadOffset_2004, the same observation for both windows). A line ends where that arm stopped managing the road. Setbacks move in whole 10 m cells; the emergent trigger fires when the setback goes below zero.
