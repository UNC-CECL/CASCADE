**`target_comparison_1996_2010_2024.png`.** The two candidate targets and the CASCADE hindcast as net change in shoreline position over each 14-yr model window by GIS domain (1 at Cape Point, 90 at Pea Island), seaward positive, domain means. Blue: the CoastSat target, the full-period 1996–2024 linear regression rate (the same rate in both windows), multiplied by 14 yr. Red: the dune-line target, the measured net change between the digitized lines (1997-10-12 to 2009-05-30 (11.6 yr) for 1996–2010; 2009-05-30 to 2023-07-01 (14.1 yr) for 2010–2024; the 2023 date assumed) divided by its interval and multiplied by 14 yr. Black: the model's own net change over the window, the edgeBE run with its ends solved on CoastSat, full management, groin off. The space between the two targets is the beach-width change they imply: solid grey where the beach widened, hatched where it narrowed. Interior GIS 2–89, model minus target: 1996–2010: against CoastSat bias -6.3 m, RMSE 21.4 m; against the dune line bias +15.0 m, RMSE 34.0 m. 2010–2024: against CoastSat bias -8.9 m, RMSE 24.9 m; against the dune line bias -8.4 m, RMSE 26.6 m. The LOESS-smoothed scores, as the runs are graded, are in tables/skill.csv. One y axis, ±80 m, the same on every figure here. Beyond ±80 m, off the axis: 1996–2010 dune-line target -83 m at GIS 12.
