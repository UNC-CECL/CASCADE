# Captions — smoothed_loess7_with_cascade

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`loess7_projected_vs_duneline_with_cascade_1996_2010_2024.png`.** The two candidate targets and the CASCADE hindcast by GIS domain (1 at Cape Point, 90 at Pea Island), 1996–2010 above 2010–2024, all three passed through the same alongshore LOESS at 7 domains (3.5 km). Shoreline is the SAME CoastSat LRR 1996–2024 × 14 yr in both panels; the dune line is always that half's own measured net change. **Dark green is CASCADE**: the zeroBE run of the matrix cell — full management, groin off — which carries NO source/sink term in ANY domain, the two ends included, so all 90 domains are the model's own response and neither target was fitted anywhere in it. That is what makes it readable against both. The coloured line and fill are the shoreline target, the black line the dune-line target, and the faint dots behind them the raw domain means. GIS 1–10 keep their raw values on every curve — the Oregon Inlet boundary treatment — so nothing there is the smoother's doing. The observed targets are smoothed at transect resolution and the model only exists per domain, so its LOESS runs over 90 points rather than ~900; at 3.5 km that barely changes the fitted curve, but it is not literally the same operation. Interior means: 1996–2010 model − shoreline -10.8 m, model − dune line +6.6 m; 2010–2024 model − shoreline -14.8 m, model − dune line -14.5 m. Seaward positive, in metres; the y axis is ±100 m. Black bars above the panel mark the beach fills placed inside the window, at the footprint the hindcast uses (2014 at GIS 84–89; 2022 at GIS 6–15; 2022 at GIS 21–28). Hatched amber boxes mark the offshore shoals (Avon Shoals GIS 24–39; Wimble Shoals GIS 60–74). Village spans are shaded; the solid hairline is the Buxton groin and the dotted hairlines are the Avon and Rodanthe piers.
