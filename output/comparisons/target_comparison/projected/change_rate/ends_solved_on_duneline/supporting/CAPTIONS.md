Blue: the CoastSat target, the full-period 1996–2024 linear regression rate (the same rate in both windows). Red: the dune-line target, the measured net change between the digitized lines (1997-10-12 to 2009-05-30 (11.6 yr) for 1996–2010; 2009-05-30 to 2023-07-01 (14.1 yr) for 2010–2024; the 2023 date assumed) divided by its interval. Black: the model's own endpoint rate (last annual shoreline minus first, over the 14 run years), the edgeBE run with its ends solved on the dune line, full management, groin off. The space between the two targets is the beach-width change they imply: solid grey where the beach widened, hatched where it narrowed. Interior GIS 2–89, model minus target: 1996–2010: against CoastSat bias -0.48 m/yr, RMSE 1.36 m/yr; against the dune line bias +1.04 m/yr, RMSE 1.97 m/yr. 2010–2024: against CoastSat bias -0.34 m/yr, RMSE 1.51 m/yr; against the dune line bias -0.30 m/yr, RMSE 1.58 m/yr.
