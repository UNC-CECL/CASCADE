# Captions — paired_smoothed

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`target_and_own_run_projected_rate_1996_2010_smoothed.png`.** 1996–2010: each candidate target with the CASCADE run calibrated to it, as shoreline change rate over the 14-yr model window by GIS domain (1 at Cape Point, 90 at Pea Island), seaward positive; the targets SMOOTHED as the runs are graded: the raw domain means over GIS 1–10 and a 7-domain LOWESS of the transect values beyond, drawn as the fill, with the raw domain means as dots. (a) The CoastSat target, the full-period 1996–2024 linear regression rate (the same rate in both windows), as the fill (blue seaward, red landward), and the edgeBE run whose two end domains were solved against it (black). (b) The dune-line target, the measured net change between the digitized lines (1997-10-12 to 2009-05-30, 11.6 yr) divided by its interval, as the fill, and the edgeBE run whose end domains were solved against the dune line (black). The model lines are each run's own endpoint rate, unchanged; the gap between line and fill is the misfit. THE ONLY SOURCE/SINK CORRECTION IN EITHER RUN IS THE BOUNDARY TERM AT GIS 1 AND GIS 90, whose values are in the figure title; every domain from GIS 2 to 89 carries none, so the interior is the model's own response. Full management, groin off. Interior GIS 2–89, model minus its own smoothed target: (a) -0.42 m/yr bias, 1.10 m/yr RMSE; (b) +1.00 m/yr bias, 1.72 m/yr RMSE. The y axis (±8 m/yr) is the same on every m/yr figure in target_comparison. Scores against the other target, raw and smoothed, are in tables/skill.csv.
