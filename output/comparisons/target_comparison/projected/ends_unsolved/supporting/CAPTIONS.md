# Captions — ends_unsolved

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`target_comparison_projected_ends_unsolved_1996_2010_2024.png`.** The two candidate targets and the CASCADE hindcast as net change in shoreline position over each 14-yr model window by GIS domain (1 at Cape Point, 90 at Pea Island), seaward positive, domain means. Blue: the CoastSat target, the full-period 1996–2024 linear regression rate (the same rate in both windows), multiplied by 14 yr. Red: the dune-line target, the measured net change between the digitized lines (1997-10-12 to 2009-05-30 (11.6 yr) for 1996–2010; 2009-05-30 to 2023-07-01 (14.1 yr) for 2010–2024; the 2023 date assumed) divided by its interval and multiplied by 14 yr. Black: the model's own net change over the window, the zeroBE run of the same matrix cell, which carries no source/sink term in any domain including the two ends, full management, groin off. The space between the two targets is the beach-width change they imply: solid grey where the beach widened, hatched where it narrowed. Interior GIS 2–89, model minus target: 1996–2010: against CoastSat bias -8.4 m, RMSE 20.0 m; against the dune line bias +12.9 m, RMSE 27.1 m. 2010–2024: against CoastSat bias -6.7 m, RMSE 22.3 m; against the dune line bias -6.2 m, RMSE 23.3 m. The LOESS-smoothed scores, as the runs are graded, are in tables/skill.csv. One y axis, ±100 m, the same on every figure here.

**`unsolved_run_and_targets_projected_1996_2010.png`.** 1996–2010: the UNCALIBRATED CASCADE run against both candidate targets, as net change in shoreline position over the 14-yr model window by GIS domain (1 at Cape Point, 90 at Pea Island), seaward positive; domain means. (a) The CoastSat target, the full-period 1996–2024 linear regression rate (the same rate in both windows) x 14 yr, as the fill (blue seaward, red landward). (b) The dune-line target, the measured net change between the digitized lines (1997-10-12 to 2009-05-30, 11.6 yr) divided by its interval and x 14 yr, as the fill. THE BLACK LINE IS THE SAME RUN IN BOTH PANELS: the zeroBE arm of the matrix cell, which carries NO source/sink term in ANY domain, the two ends included, so every one of the 90 domains is the model's own response and neither target was fitted. Full management, groin off. The gap between line and fill is the misfit. Interior GIS 2–89, model minus target: (a) -8.4 m bias, 20.0 m RMSE; (b) +12.9 m bias, 27.1 m RMSE. The y axis (±100 m) is the same on every figure in target_comparison. Scores for every model set against every target, raw and smoothed, are in tables/skill.csv.
