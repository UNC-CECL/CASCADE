**`target_comparison_total_change_ends_solved_on_duneline_1996_2010_2024.png`.** The two candidate targets and the CASCADE hindcast as net change in shoreline position over each 14-yr model window by GIS domain (1 at Cape Point, 90 at Pea Island), seaward positive, domain means. Blue: the CoastSat target, the window's own linear regression rate (the runner's scoring series), multiplied by 14 yr. Red: the dune-line target, the measured net change between the digitized lines (1997-10-12 to 2009-05-30 (11.6 yr) for 1996–2010; 2009-05-30 to 2023-07-01 (14.1 yr) for 2010–2024; the 2023 date assumed) divided by its interval and multiplied by 14 yr. Black: the model's own net change over the window, the edgeBE run with its ends solved on the dune line, full management, groin off. The space between the two targets is the beach-width change they imply: solid grey where the beach widened, hatched where it narrowed. Interior GIS 2–89, model minus target: 1996–2010: against CoastSat bias -6.3 m, RMSE 26.8 m; against the dune line bias +8.4 m, RMSE 26.2 m. 2010–2024: against CoastSat bias -24.5 m, RMSE 36.4 m; against the dune line bias -10.6 m, RMSE 26.1 m. The LOESS-smoothed scores, as the runs are graded, are in tables/skill.csv. One y axis, ±100 m, the same on every figure here.
