# Captions — means

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`coastsat_means_1984_2004.png`.** Observed CoastSat shoreline change rate and modelled shoreline change rate by GIS domain (1 at Cape Point, 90 at Pea Island) for 1984–2004. The observed line is the mean linear regression rate of the CoastSat transects inside each 500 m domain, blue and filled where the shoreline moved seaward, red where it moved landward; the dotted lines are ±1 standard deviation across those transects. The black line is the modelled shoreline change of the same window, the linear regression rate, the OLS slope over the run's annual shorelines, the estimator the CoastSat comparison and the run index use, from the edgeBE source/sink preset under full management with the groin off with the two end domains solved against the dune-line change (mean3 reading, endpoint estimator; experiments/2026-09-16-dune-edgesolve) instead of CoastSat: 1984–2004: HAT_1984_2004_edgeBE_road_bdm_nogroin (2026-09-16-dune-edgesolve/mean3/step2 arm). Village spans are shaded; the solid hairline is the Buxton groin and the dotted hairlines are the Avon and Rodanthe piers. The y axis is held at ±10 m/yr on every panel, the largest |rate| over every observed reading and modelled curve of every window plus 1 m rounded up, so the panels are directly comparable.
