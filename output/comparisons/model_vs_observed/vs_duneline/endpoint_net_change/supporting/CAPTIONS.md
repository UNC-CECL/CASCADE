# Captions — endpoint_net_change

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`model_vs_duneline_netchange_1984_2004.png`.** Observed dune-line change and modelled shoreline change rate by GIS domain (1 at Cape Point, 90 at Pea Island) for 1984–2004. The observed line is the digitised dune line at the window's end vintage minus the line at its start vintage, per 500 m domain (mean over its ~5 transects, first station per transect, as the hindcast's end-year target loader reads the raw offsets), divided by the interval between the two survey dates, blue and filled where the dune line moved seaward and red where it moved landward. Vintages and dates: 1984–2004: the 1984 line (1984-09-19) to the 2004 line (2004-05-25), 19.68 yr. The black line is the modelled shoreline change of the same window, the endpoint rate, the run's last annual shoreline minus its first over the run years, the like-for-like estimator for two surveys, from the edgeBE source/sink preset under full management with the groin off with the two end domains solved against the dune-line change (mean3 reading, endpoint estimator; experiments/2026-09-16-dune-edgesolve) instead of CoastSat: 1984–2004: HAT_1984_2004_edgeBE_road_bdm_nogroin (2026-09-16-dune-edgesolve/mean3/step2 arm). A dune line and a shoreline are different features, so a gap between the two curves is beach-width change as much as model misfit. Village spans are shaded; the solid hairline is the Buxton groin and the dotted hairlines are the Avon and Rodanthe piers. The y axis is held at ±10 m/yr on every panel, the largest |rate| over every observed reading and modelled curve of every window plus 1 m rounded up, so the panels are directly comparable.
