# Captions — net_change

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`model_vs_shoreline_and_duneline_netchange_1984_2004.png`.** The two scoring targets and modelled net change in shoreline position by GIS domain (1 at Cape Point, 90 at Pea Island) for 1984–2004. The two coloured lines are the two observations as net change in position, each measured between the same two dune-line dates, divided by that survey interval and multiplied by the window's calendar years, so they sit on the model's span (the same scaling as target_comparison), then given the scoring target's treatment (a 7-domain LOWESS of the transect rates north of domain 10, and the raw domain means over domains 1–10 where the Oregon Inlet boundary dominates). Blue is the CoastSat shoreline: the mean satellite position within six months of each dune-line date, differenced (5-scr/3-rates/coastsat/endpoint). Red is the digitised dune line, end line minus start line (5-scr/3-rates/duneline/endpoint). Vintages, dates and survey intervals: 1984–2004: the 1984 line (1984-09-19) to the 2004 line (2004-05-25), 19.68 yr. Where the interval is shorter than the window the scaling assumes the same rate over the missing years. The gap between the two lines is beach-width change, which the model, whose shoreline is a dune line behind a fixed berm, cannot represent. The same figure in m/yr is under change_rate/. The two black lines are the modelled shoreline change of the same window, its net change, the run's last annual shoreline minus its first (the endpoint rate x the run years, 14 in 1996–2010 and 2010–2024), from the edgeBE source/sink preset under full management with the groin off, each against the target its two end domains were solved on: solid with the two end domains solved against the CoastSat target (); dashed with the two end domains solved against the dune-line change (mean3 reading, endpoint estimator; experiments/2026-09-16-dune-edgesolve) instead of CoastSat (). No run exists yet for 1984–2004; that panel shows the observation alone. A dune line and a shoreline are different features, so a gap between the two curves is beach-width change as much as model misfit. Village spans are shaded; the solid hairline is the Buxton groin and the dotted hairlines are the Avon and Rodanthe piers. The y axis is held at ±125 m on every panel, the largest |net change| over both observations and every modelled curve of every window plus 5 m rounded up to 5 m, so the panels are directly comparable; the 1984–2004 and 2004–2024 windows span 20 yr and the other two 14 yr, so equal rates draw larger there.
