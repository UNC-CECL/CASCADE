**`groin_effect_buxton_2009_2025.png`.** Shoreline position change at the cape end of Hatteras Island, GIS domains 1–10, 2009–2025 (test period); positive is seaward. The layout of the run's shoreline_position_change_with_buffers figure, cut to these domains with the y axis at ±55 m. Modelled (CASCADE): the shoreline at the end of the run minus the start, per 500 m domain (unsmoothed here, as south of domain 10 in every figure), for the full-management run (road maintenance, beach and dune management, nourishment, source/sink at the end domains only) with the Buxton groin (orange, solid) and without it (grey, dashed); everything else is the same. The groin's effect is largest at GIS 3, -2.2 m. Both runs use the same edgeBE end value at GIS 1, solved with the groin on, so near GIS 1 the no-groin line is the groin removed with the ends held, not a separately fitted no-groin model. Observed (CoastSat, blue): per transect, the mean position over 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31 minus the mean over 2009-01-01 to 2009-12-31 (dots, each centred on its own domain), and the mean of each domain's transects (line). Shaded band: Buxton; dotted: the groin field, between domains 5 and 6; dashed at 10.5: where the observed LOWESS begins. Beyond ±55 m, off the axis and marked with a triangle at the edge: CASCADE, groin +160 m at GIS 1; CASCADE, no groin +159 m at GIS 1. Runs HAT_2009_2025_edgeBE_offsetmetres_road_bdm_nourish_groin and HAT_2009_2025_edgeBE_offsetmetres_road_bdm_nourish_nogroin.
