# Captions — figures

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`straight_coast_1-source-sink-dipole_profiles.png`.** **Tests:** what the source/sink (dipole) groin does on its own, on a straight coast with nothing else going on, and whether it behaves like a groin: sand held on the updrift side, taken from the downdrift side, and no sand created or destroyed. **How to read:** BRIE's alongshore solve alone (option A waves; no storms, no Barrier3D, no sea-level rise), one groin between the two middle domains. Each panel is one shoreline orientation; lines are the shoreline change from the straight start at years 1, 5, 10, 25 and 50 (light to dark), seaward positive, with the updrift side on the right; the arrow is the wave-climate net longshore drift on the undisturbed coast, which runs toward the groin from the right. A groin that traps drift builds out on the right and cuts back on the left. Red line: the groin face. **Shows:** after 50 years the cell just updrift has moved +58 m at -30$^\circ$, +37 m at 0$^\circ$ and +7 m at +30$^\circ$; the module's own sand balance over 50 years ranges from -0 to -0 m of summed shoreline across orientations, and the total change in the reach (module plus solve) from -1481 to +753 m (comparison figures, and tables/).
