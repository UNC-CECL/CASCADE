# Captions — figures

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`straight_coast_modules_vs_orientation.png`.** Four groin modules on a straight coast at orientations -40 to +40 degrees, 50 years of BRIE's alongshore solve alone (option A waves). (a) The wave-climate net drift (solid) and BRIE's diffusivity (dashed; BRIE clips negative values to zero, which freezes the coast). (b) Seaward change of the updrift cell beside the groin. (c) Sand the module itself added, as summed shoreline change across the reach (zero for a conserving module). (d) Sand BRIE's row-scaled solve added on top, which is non-zero wherever the groin makes the diffusivity vary along the coast. Dipole M 12; trapping b 0.6.

**`straight_coast_profiles_year50.png`.** The four groin modules after 50 years on a straight coast at six orientations, BRIE's alongshore solve alone (option A waves). Shoreline change from the straight start, seaward positive, updrift side on the right; red line, the groin face; each panel has its own y range.
