# Captions — figures

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`HAT_gapfill_2009-2014_island.png`.** The 2009 DEM gap fill, every domain on one 10 m grid. (a) the 2009 survey alone, 314,427 cells; (b) the surface the extractor reads, 569,187 cells (+254,760); (c) the survey each cell came from. Domain 1 is at Cape Point in the south, domain 90 at Pea Island in the north. Fill is limited to cells the 2014 source measured, contiguous with the island (20 m bridging), above -2.64 m NAVD88. No bias correction, no feathering — filled cells are the 2014 measurement unchanged. Fill source: 2014 NOAA Post-Sandy DEM (Job1076021), 1 m, EPSG:6347 + NAVD88. Axes are UTM eastings and northings in km; domain outlines are white.
