# Captions — figures

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`storm_record_1996_2024.png`.** Storm record for the 1996–2024 hindcast: the v3_split12_trim24 series the model runs on (329 events; 1996–2009 from the 1996–2010 file, 2010–2024 from the 2010–2024 file, the calendar years each run spends). Events are hours when total water level (Duck gauge 8651370 + Stockdon 2006 R2% from WIS 63228 waves, beach slope 0.06) exceeds the berm (1.7 m NAVD88 = 1.34 m MHW), grouped when <24 h apart, kept when ≥8 h; each group split where the water stays below the berm ≥12 h, 144 events longer than 24 h reach the model as the 24 h around their peak. Dark orange: a tropical or subtropical cyclone in the NHC best tracks (HURDAT2) was within 500 km of Cape Hatteras within 24 h of the event's peak (31 events). Slate: every other event (298), mostly nor'easters (229 of them peak October–April), but identified only by the absence of a nearby cyclone. Water levels are the model's estimate from the gauge and hindcast waves, not observations at Hatteras. Each event is placed at the hour of its peak total water level. (a) Events per calendar year; dashed lines are each period's mean (9.9 yr⁻¹ 1996–2009, 12.7 yr⁻¹ 2010–2024). (b) Rhigh of every event (m above MHW; add 0.36 m for NAVD88). The dashed vertical line is the 2010 boundary between the two hindcast runs. Every tropical cyclone is labelled with its HURDAT2 name, at its highest event where it made two; the other events have no official name and are unlabelled, except the highest, the nor'easter of March 2018.

**`storm_characteristics_1996_2024.png`.** The two hindcast periods' storm series compared (v3_split12_trim24; 1996–2009 as spent by the 1996–2010 run, red, 9.9 events/yr; 2010–2024 as spent by the 2010–2024 run, blue, 12.7 events/yr). Every panel is normalised per year so the 14- and 15-year periods compare. (a) Exceedance: events per year whose Rhigh reaches the value on the x-axis, from the berm (1.34 m MHW) up; log scale. (b) Events per year by the month of the peak. (c) Length of each event above the berm as it came out of grouping, before the 24 h trim (bins widen past 48 h; the last bin holds everything ≥144 h). Events right of the dashed line reach the model as the 24 h around their peak. (d) Rhigh against the event's wave period (Tp, the value the model receives).
