# Captions — shorelines

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`nourishment_shorelines_2014_rodanthe.png`.** Rodanthe emergency fill, 2014, in CoastSat. (a) The median shoreline over 2013-07-29 to 2014-07-28 (red, before placement) and over 2014-09-21 to 2015-09-20 (blue, after), each transect's position measured from a degree-2 baseline fitted through the before line, so the coast is straightened and the cross-shore axis is stretched; blue shading is seaward gain, red landward loss. (b) After minus before at each transect; the dashed line is the median change of the transects outside every fill footprint, and the bracket the half-peak core from nourishment_extent_coastsat.py. (c) Both lines on Esri World Imagery at true scale, centred on the largest 5-transect running-median gain inside the footprint (GIS 85); the imagery is current, not of either window. Grey columns are the model footprint (HATTERAS_NOURISHMENT_PROJECTS).
