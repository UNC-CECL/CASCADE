# Captions — 2-model-input

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`nourishment_when_where.png`.** Beach nourishment on the modelled reach, 1984–2024, by year placed (vertical) and GIS domain (horizontal, south to north, 500 m each). Each filled bar is one project as the hindcast receives it from hatteras_site_config.HATTERAS_NOURISHMENT_PROJECTS: Rodanthe emergency fill 2014, domains 82–88 (7 domains, 3.5 km, 1.6 M cy); Buxton beach nourishment 2017, domains 6–16 (11 domains, 5.5 km, 2.6 M cy); Buxton shore protection 2022, domains 6–16 (11 domains, 5.5 km, 1.2 M cy); Avon shore protection 2022, domains 21–28 (8 domains, 4.0 km, 1.0 M cy). Colour is the year placed, red the earliest and blue the latest. The dark inner bar is the footprint flagged for the same project in Hatteras_Management_Timelines.xlsx (Nourishment_Timeline sheet): 2014 domains 85–88; 2022 domains 6–15; 2022 domains 23–26. Where the two differ the site-config extent was re-derived from the project description (Rodanthe: 2 mi north of the village, stopping short of the locked boundary domain 90; Avon: Due East Road to Askins Creek North Drive) and the record's flags are kept as drawn, not reconciled. The record flags nothing for Buxton beach nourishment 2017: the project is missing from it (nourishment/1-sources/README.md), so it has no inner bar. Hollow markers at the right are the 15 Pea Island / Oregon Inlet navigation and emergency fills the record lists (1990, 1991, 1992, 1993, 1995, 1996, 1997, 1998, 1999, 2000, 2001, 2002, 2003, 2004, 2013); they lie north of domain 90 and no run sees them. Brackets at the left are the 4 hindcast windows; a run fires whatever falls inside its window, so 1984–2004 and 1996–2009 carry no fill, 2004–2024 and 2009–2025 carry all 4. Named bands along the foot are the villages.

**`nourishment_volume_alongshore.png`.** Nourishment volume per metre of shoreline that each GIS domain receives in the hindcast, by year placed. A project's reported total (cubic yards, from the permitting record) is converted to cubic metres and spread evenly over its domains of 500 m: Rodanthe emergency fill (2014): 1.6 M cy = 1.24 M m³ over 7 domains, 354 m³/m; Buxton beach nourishment (2017): 2.6 M cy = 1.99 M m³ over 11 domains, 361 m³/m; Buxton shore protection (2022): 1.2 M cy = 0.92 M m³ over 11 domains, 167 m³/m; Avon shore protection (2022): 1.0 M cy = 0.76 M m³ over 8 domains, 191 m³/m. Even spreading is an assumption; real fill templates taper at their ends. Bars are stacked by year placed: domains 6–16 are filled twice (2017, 2022), so the top of a stack is the 1984–2024 cumulative, 528 m³/m at most. The densest fill, Buxton 2017, put 2.2 times as much sand per metre as the thinnest, Buxton 2022. The dotted line is the Buxton groin field, from which the Buxton footprint was measured north; triangles are the piers, and the Avon footprint was placed about the pier. Named bands along the top are the villages: the Buxton and Rodanthe footprints extend out of their villages into the road corridor, the Avon footprint stays inside its village.
