# Captions — nourishment

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`nourishment_when_where.png`.** Beach nourishment on the modelled reach, 1984–2024, by year placed (vertical) and GIS domain (horizontal, south to north, 500 m each). Each filled bar is one project as the hindcast receives it from hatteras_site_config.HATTERAS_NOURISHMENT_PROJECTS: Rodanthe emergency fill 2014, domains 84–89 (6 domains, 3.0 km, 1.6 M cy); Buxton shore protection 2022, domains 6–15 (10 domains, 5.0 km, 1.2 M cy); Avon shore protection 2022, domains 21–28 (8 domains, 4.0 km, 1.0 M cy). Red is the earlier fill, blue the later ones. The dark inner bar is the footprint flagged for the same project in Hatteras_Management_Timelines.xlsx (Nourishment_Timeline sheet): 2014 domains 85–88; 2022 domains 6–15; 2022 domains 23–26. Where the two differ the site-config extent was re-derived from the project description (Rodanthe: 2 mi north of the village, stopping short of the locked boundary domain 90; Avon: Due East Road to Askins Creek North Drive) and the record's flags are kept as drawn, not reconciled. Hollow markers at the right are the 15 Pea Island / Oregon Inlet navigation and emergency fills the record lists (1990, 1991, 1992, 1993, 1995, 1996, 1997, 1998, 1999, 2000, 2001, 2002, 2003, 2004, 2013); they lie north of domain 90 and no run sees them. Brackets at the left are the 4 hindcast windows; a run fires whatever falls inside its window, so 1984–2004 and 1996–2010 carry no fill, 2004–2024 and 2010–2024 carry all 3. Named bands along the foot are the villages.

**`nourishment_volume_alongshore.png`.** Nourishment volume per metre of shoreline that each GIS domain receives in the hindcast, by year placed. A project's reported total (cubic yards, from the permitting record) is converted to cubic metres and spread evenly over its domains of 500 m: Rodanthe emergency fill (2014): 1.6 M cy = 1.22 M m³ over 6 domains, 408 m³/m; Buxton shore protection (2022): 1.2 M cy = 0.92 M m³ over 10 domains, 183 m³/m; Avon shore protection (2022): 1.0 M cy = 0.76 M m³ over 8 domains, 191 m³/m. Even spreading is an assumption; real fill templates taper at their ends. No domain is filled twice, so the bars are also the 1984–2024 cumulative. The 2014 Rodanthe fill is about twice as dense as the 2022 fills because it put more sand into a shorter reach. The dotted line is the Buxton groin field, from which the Buxton footprint was measured north; triangles are the piers, and the Avon footprint was placed about the pier. Named bands along the top are the villages: the Buxton and Rodanthe footprints extend out of their villages into the road corridor, the Avon footprint stays inside its village.

**`nourishment_domain_map.png`.** The 90 GIS domains in place, the island rotated 90° clockwise so that south is to the left, north to the right and the Atlantic at the bottom; rotation preserves distance, so the scale bar holds. The 24 domains that receive a nourishment project in the hindcast are filled by the year it was placed, with the site-config extents of Figure 1; grey is the island outline and white domains are never nourished. Every tenth domain and the ends of each footprint are numbered along the top. The Pea Island / Oregon Inlet fills in the management record lie beyond the north end of the strip.
