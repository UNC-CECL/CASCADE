# Captions — 3-maps

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`nourishment_domain_map.png`.** The 90 GIS domains in place, the island rotated 90° clockwise so that south is to the left, north to the right and the Atlantic at the bottom; rotation preserves distance, so the scale bar holds. The 26 domains that receive a nourishment project in the hindcast are filled by community, in the colours of the project maps, with the site-config extents of Figure 1; grey is the island outline and white domains are never nourished. A domain filled more than once is split across the island into one band per fill, the earliest on the ocean side: domains 6–16 are filled twice (2017, 2022). Every tenth domain and the ends of each footprint are numbered along the top. The Pea Island / Oregon Inlet fills in the management record lie beyond the north end of the strip.

**`nourishment_maps_paper.png`.** Beach nourishment projects applied in the hindcast, north up. The shaded outline in each panel is the fill footprint (colour marks the community) and thin white lines its 500 m model domains; the first and last domains are numbered. The 2017 and 2022 Buxton fills share one footprint and one panel. Each reported volume is spread evenly over its footprint. Red lines are the Buxton groins, white lines the piers. All three panels share one scale. The left panel boxes each footprint, (a)–(c), on Hatteras Island. Imagery: Esri World Imagery, accessed 2026-10-04, not of the fill year. (a) Rodanthe emergency fill, 2014: GIS 82–88 (3.5 km), 1.62 × 10⁶ yd³ (1.24 × 10⁶ m³, 354 m³ m⁻¹), placed 2014-07-29 to 2014-09-20; (b) Avon shore protection, 2022: GIS 21–28 (4.0 km), 1.00 × 10⁶ yd³ (0.76 × 10⁶ m³, 191 m³ m⁻¹), placed 2022-06-19 to 2022-07-26; (c) Buxton beach nourishment, 2017: GIS 6–16 (5.5 km), 2.60 × 10⁶ yd³ (1.99 × 10⁶ m³, 361 m³ m⁻¹), placed 2017-06-21 to 2018-02-27; Buxton shore protection, 2022: GIS 6–16 (5.5 km), 1.20 × 10⁶ yd³ (0.92 × 10⁶ m³, 167 m³ m⁻¹), placed 2022-06-30 to 2022-08-16. Reported extents and sources: nourishment_summary_table.
