# Captions — dunestart_offset

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`HAT_dunestart_road_on_domains.png`.** NC-12 placed on the Barrier3D interiors CASCADE initialises with, from the setback measured landward from the dune start. Domain 1 is at Cape Point in the south and domain 90 at Pea Island in the north; the shaded spans are the villages (Buxton, Avon, Tri-Village). (a, b) the road as roadway_manager.bulldoze places it, road_start = int(setback / 10 m), on each period's OWN extraction — 1984 on 1984-start/v2, 2004 on 2004-start/v1. These are different islands: 65 of 90 domains differ in interior shape, so the two panels are not one island drawn twice. Interior elevation is shown in classes relative to mean high water; cells outside the extraction carry no data and are drawn grey. (c) the same setback against the island width it has to fit inside. (d) the change in setback between the two periods. Median change between the periods +0 m; bars above zero are further inland by 2004, below zero closer to the dune. (e) bulldoze's own drown test: the wetter of the two rows BORDERING the bulldozed band (road_start − 1, road_end + 1). Above 20% of bordering cells at or below 0 m MHW CASCADE stops managing the roadway, and the road is drawn in the accent colour wherever that happens — 0 of 82 in 1984; 0 of 82 in 2004. The setback was measured in the same frame it is drawn in, interior row 0, so the drawing and the measurement agree. 2 domain-year(s) had a negative true setback and were floored to 0, putting the road on interior row 0.
**`HAT_road_island_planview_1984.png`.** NC-12 on Hatteras Island as CASCADE initialises it, 1984 vintage. Domain 1 is at Cape Point in the south, domain 90 at Pea Island in the north. Barrier interior and dune from the 1984-start/v2 extraction, assembled in the alongshore-offset frame with every domain padded to 200 cells / 2000 m cross-shore; colour is elevation relative to mean high water. NC-12 is drawn as Barrier3D represents it (roadway_manager.py:99): per domain a single flat band, int(setback/10 m) cells landward of interior row 0, 2 cells (20 m) wide, spanning all 50 alongshore profiles at one constant elevation — so it steps between domains rather than following the surveyed centreline. Road colour is elevation from RoadElevation.csv, a single file used for both vintages, so road colour is identical between the 1984 and 2004 figures by construction and any difference between them is the setback or the island, never the roadbed; the dashed mark on that scale is the 1.34 m berm. 82 of 90 domains carry road; domains 1–8 carry none and are hatched along the lower frame. The road band is stroked at 1.6 pt against a true width of 0.4 pt so that it can carry colour; cross-shore position and alongshore extent are exact. Vertical exaggeration ×2.6. Sources: 1984-start/v2; setback RoadSetback_1984_dunestart.csv; elevation RoadElevation.csv; offsets Island_Dune_Offsets_1984_CASCADE_Input.csv; drawn by HAT_road_island_planview.py on the terrain ramp.
