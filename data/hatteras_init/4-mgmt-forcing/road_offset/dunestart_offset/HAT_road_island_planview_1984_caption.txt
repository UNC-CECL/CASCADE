NC-12 on Hatteras Island as CASCADE initialises it, 1984 vintage. Barrier interior and dune from the 1984-start/v1 extraction, assembled in the alongshore-offset frame with every domain padded to 200 cells / 2000 m cross-shore; colour is elevation relative to mean high water. NC-12 is drawn as Barrier3D represents it (roadway_manager.py:99): per domain a single flat band, int(setback/10 m) cells landward of interior row 0, 2 cells (20 m) wide, spanning all 50 alongshore profiles at one constant elevation — so it steps between domains rather than following the surveyed centreline. Road colour is elevation from RoadElevation.csv, a single file used for both vintages, so road colour is identical between the 1984 and 2004 figures by construction and any difference between them is the setback or the island, never the roadbed; the dashed mark on that scale is the 1.34 m berm. 82 of 90 domains carry road; domains 1–8 carry none and are hatched along the lower frame. The road band is stroked at 1.6 pt against a true width of 0.8 pt so that it can carry colour; cross-shore position and alongshore extent are exact. Vertical exaggeration ×1.5.
