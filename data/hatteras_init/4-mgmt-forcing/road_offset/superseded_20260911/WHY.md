# old_method_offset — retired

The LEGACY road setback method: measured against the same-year digitised dune line rather than interior row 0. Kept because the comparison between the two methods is a result in its own right, and because the published RoadSetback CSVs were made this way.

**Dated 2026-09-11**, from git: the last change before the 2026-09 reorganisation, which is the best available evidence of when work on it stopped. It is not necessarily the day a decision was taken.

Nothing here is maintained or expected to run. Rule 4 of `ORGANIZATION.md`: retirement is a dated folder with a note, and deletes nothing.
