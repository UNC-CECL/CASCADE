# Captions — road_relocation_1978_2008 figures

Written by `HAT_road_relocation_distance.py` on 2026-09-15 10:28; every number is from `road_relocation_1978_2008.csv` beside the figures. The two NC-12 centrelines were digitised from 1978 and 2008 imagery and stand in for the 1978 and 2008 hindcast starts; the interval measured is therefore about 30 years. Displacement is the shortest distance from a point on the 1978 centreline to the 2008 centreline, projected onto the landward normal (landward positive), sampled every 5 m.

**Figure 1 — `road_relocation_1978_2008_alongshore.png`.** Displacement of the NC-12 centreline, 1978–2008, per GIS domain from south (left) to north (right). Dark bars are the mean signed displacement over the samples in the domain; pale bars the largest displacement within it. Grey bands mark domains where the centreline is unchanged between the two surveys (light) or was re-digitised without moving, under 5 m (darker). 12 of 83 road-carrying domains relocated, all landward; the largest displacement is 124 m in GIS 85. Brackets name the two relocation sites; the named bands along the foot of the panel are the villages.

**Figure 2 — `road_relocation_1978_2008_sites.png`.** The two relocations at true scale, north up, both panels at one scale. (a) Buxton, domains 8–15: 8 domains, mean displacement 36 m, largest 83 m; (b) N of Rodanthe, domains 84–87: 4 domains, mean displacement 63 m, largest 124 m. The 1978 centreline is drawn dashed under its sample points, coloured by the measured landward displacement to the 2008 centreline (solid); grey points are samples where the two centrelines coincide. Each domain is labelled with its mean measured displacement and the value the model is forced with, that mean rounded to the nearest 10 m Barrier3D cell (GIS 9 18.0 → 20 m, GIS 10 46.9 → 50 m, GIS 11 77.1 → 80 m, GIS 12 68.4 → 70 m, GIS 13 50.9 → 50 m, GIS 14 24.7 → 20 m, GIS 84 39.9 → 40 m, GIS 85 108.7 → 110 m, GIS 86 93.7 → 90 m, GIS 87 17.5 → 20 m). Domains 8 (sign agreement 0.54), 15 (sign agreement 0.56) lie within the digitised change but carry no prescribed move: the two centrelines cross there, so the samples do not agree on a direction and the mean is not a displacement.

**Figure 3 — `road_relocation_1978_2008_domain_map.png`.** All 90 domains in place, the island rotated 90° clockwise so that south is to the left, north to the right and the Atlantic at the bottom; rotation preserves distance, so the scale bar holds. Relocated domains are outlined and filled by their mean displacement on the colour scale shared with Figure 2; the grey classes are those of Figure 1, and white domains carry no 1978 road. Every tenth domain and every relocated domain is numbered along the top.
