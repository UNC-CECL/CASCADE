## `overwash_heatmap_period1.png` (2-record/heatmaps)

Observed overwash on Hatteras Island, 1984–2004. (a) One row per image assessed (12 images, labelled by year and date; * poor or partial image), one column per CASCADE domain, 1 at Cape Point to 90 at Pea Island; the light bands are the villages, named under (d). Purple where overwash was present, white where the image was assessed and showed none, dotted where the image does not reach the domain, hatched rows where no image exists; runs of such years are drawn as one thin row (1986, 1988, 1990, 1994, 1999–2003). Rows are the Hapke and Henderson (2007) delineations. (b) Domains overwashed in each image, over the number assessed (grey). (c) The named storms of the reference sheet at their year, the marker giving the peak category, bold with a filled dot where the image on that row was taken after the storm (so the row can show its effects), faint italic where the image predates it or there is none that year, with a line to the first image taken after the storm; e.g. GLORIA (Oct 1985) → 10 Jul 1987; KATE (Nov 1985) → 10 Jul 1987; CHARLEY (Aug 1986) → 10 Jul 1987; ALBERTO (Aug 1988) → 30 Jul 1989; Perfect Storm (Nov 1991) → 02 Oct 1992; Dec 1992 Nor'easter (Dec 1992) → 02 Sep 1993. The rule is the first image on or after the storm's last listed day, with a 7-day grace; the May 2022 nor'easter is routed to the Oct 2023 image by hand. (d) Number of images showing each domain overwashed. 2004 belongs to both periods.

## `overwash_vs_footprint_alongshore.png` (3-vs-footprint)

Observed overwash 1985 to 1997 against the 1984 footprint, by domain (1 at Cape Point, 90 at Pea Island; the light bands are the villages, named across the top of (a)). (a) the 9 images taken after the frame the 1984 dune line was digitised on (19 Sep 1984) and up to the frame the 1997 line was digitised on (12 Oct 1997); purple where overwash was present, dotted where the image does not reach the domain, * poor image. (b) The footprint's median shift between the two dune lines with the p10 to p90 over 50 profiles, positive where the 1984 line lay seaward (rows added, red), negative where it lay landward (rows removed, blue), grey where under one 10 m cell; hatched where the spread straddles zero; right axis in 10 m rows; ▼ marks the domains with overwash in (a). (c) Flags carried, not dropped: NC-12 relocated over 1984 to 2004, the Buxton, Avon and Rodanthe S-curves erosion hotspots, the domain-6 jetties, and CoastSat 1984 to 2004 shoreline erosion faster than 2 m/yr; the straddle flag is the hatching in (b). Given overwash (27 domains): 20 rows added, 7 unchanged, 0 rows removed. Given the action: 20 of 29 rows-added and 0 of 23 rows-removed domains show overwash. A rows-added domain without overwash is not counted against the footprint: the images are two to four years apart and washover fades from imagery, so absence is weak evidence.
