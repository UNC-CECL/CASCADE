## `overwash_where_record_1996_2025.png` (2-record/1-where)

Where overwash recurred on Hatteras Island over the full model window, 1996–2025, from the image record in Hatteras_Overwash_Data.xlsx: the 19 images taken in its storm years, 1997–2025 (the Hapke and Henderson (2007) delineations (1997–1998) and Google Earth imagery (2004–2024); 3 flagged poor or partial and kept), the same images the model comparison uses. Earlier images (1984–1996) are left out. Each image records overwash present or absent per CASCADE domain. (a) The island (NC 1:80k coastline, UTM 18N, true scale) with each domain box, clipped to land, shaded by the number of images showing it overwashed; unshaded = none. Domain boxes numbered every ten; NC-12 as the dark line. (b) The same count per domain, level with the island, in the same shades. (c–e) The three most-hit reaches at true scale (Rodanthe–Pea Island, domains 82–90; Wimble Shoals, domains 45–54; Cape Point, domains 1–7), outlined in (a), with the domain boxes numbered. 83 of 90 domains were overwashed in at least one image. Most often: domain 3 (5 of 16 images); domain 4 (5 of 16 images); domain 84 (5 of 17 images); domain 86 (5 of 17 images); domain 87 (5 of 17 images); domain 2 (4 of 16 images). Each domain is covered by 15–17 of the 19 images (an image may not reach every domain), so the counts are comparable along the island. The shading is per domain: it says a domain was overwashed in an image, not where in the domain or how much of it.

## `overwash_by_image_1997_2009.png` (2-record/2-when)

Observed overwash on Hatteras Island, 1997–2009 (images taken in the model window's storm years, 1997–2009). (a) One row per image assessed (8 images, labelled by year and date; * poor or partial image), one column per CASCADE domain, 1 at Cape Point to 90 at Pea Island; the light bands are the villages, named under (d). Purple where overwash was present, white where the image was assessed and showed none, dotted where the image does not reach the domain, hatched rows where no image exists; runs of such years are drawn as one thin row (1999–2003, 2007). Rows are the Hapke and Henderson (2007) delineations (1997–1998) and Google Earth imagery (2004–2009). (b) Domains overwashed in each image, over the number assessed (grey). (c) The named storms of the reference sheet at their year, the marker giving the peak category, bold with a filled dot where the image on that row was taken after the storm (so the row can show its effects), faint italic where the image predates it or there is none that year, with a line to the first image taken after the storm; e.g. DENNIS (Sep 1999) → 25 May 2004; IRENE (Oct 1999) → 25 May 2004; KYLE (Oct 2002) → 25 May 2004; ISABEL (Sep 2003) → 25 May 2004; ALEX (Aug 2004) → 01 Sep 2005; OPHELIA (Sep 2005) → 30 Jun 2006. The rule is the first image on or after the storm's last listed day, with a 7-day grace; the May 2022 nor'easter is routed to the Oct 2023 image by hand. (d) Number of images showing each domain overwashed.

## `overwash_vs_footprint_alongshore.png` (3-vs-footprint)

Observed overwash 1985 to 1997 against the 1984 footprint, by domain (1 at Cape Point, 90 at Pea Island; the light bands are the villages, named across the top of (a)). (a) the 9 images taken after the frame the 1984 dune line was digitised on (19 Sep 1984) and up to the frame the 1997 line was digitised on (12 Oct 1997); purple where overwash was present, dotted where the image does not reach the domain, * poor image. (b) The footprint's median shift between the two dune lines with the p10 to p90 over 50 profiles, positive where the 1984 line lay seaward (rows added, red), negative where it lay landward (rows removed, blue), grey where under one 10 m cell; hatched where the spread straddles zero; right axis in 10 m rows; ▼ marks the domains with overwash in (a). (c) Flags carried, not dropped: NC-12 relocated over 1984 to 2004, the Buxton, Avon and Rodanthe S-curves erosion hotspots, the domain-6 jetties, and CoastSat 1984 to 2004 shoreline erosion faster than 2 m/yr; the straddle flag is the hatching in (b). Given overwash (27 domains): 20 rows added, 7 unchanged, 0 rows removed. Given the action: 20 of 29 rows-added and 0 of 23 rows-removed domains show overwash. A rows-added domain without overwash is not counted against the footprint: the images are two to four years apart and washover fades from imagery, so absence is weak evidence.
