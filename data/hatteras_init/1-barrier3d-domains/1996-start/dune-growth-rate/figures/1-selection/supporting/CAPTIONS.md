# Captions — 1-selection

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`stable_dune_fig1_where.png`.** Where the dunes were stable, 1996-2009. (a) Shoreline change rate per domain (CoastSat linear regression, 1996-2009); dark bars lie within +/-0.5 m/yr. (b) Change in the domain-median dune crest between the 1996 and 2009 lidar surveys, on the 10 m cells with a dune in all nine surveys. (c) Number of imagery dates showing overwash. Shaded: GIS 20-22 (Buxton-Avon / Avon) and GIS 59-62 (Mid-island / Wimble Shoals), where the shoreline held, no overwash was seen and the dune changed gently; these calibrate the growth rate. Producer: HAT_stable_dune_figures.py.

**`stable_dune_fig3_excluded.png`.** As stable_dune_fig2 at two domains left out of the calibration. GIS 44: the beach built out (+1.7 m/yr) and the dune grew 2 m, fed by the extra sand, which would overstate r. GIS 65: the shoreline retreated (-2.2 m/yr); the crest held while the dune lost width, which r cannot represent. Producer: HAT_stable_dune_figures.py.
