CAVEATS - read before using the footprint
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. THE ADDED ROWS ARE FABRICATED. No survey covers land that had eroded
     away by 1996. This report sizes the block; it does not fill it.
  2. THE REMOVED ROWS ARE SURVEYED. Where the island prograded, rows
     0..|N|-1 are 1996 beach and foredune that were ocean in 1984;
     removing them deletes measured cells on the strength of the line pair.
  3. THE LINE IS 1997, THE DEM's BEACH IS 1996. One year of change is
     unaccounted for. Recorded, not corrected.
  4. THE TWO LINES MAY NOT BE THE SAME FEATURE. duneline_1997 records
     'light/dark elevation break'; duneline_1984 carries no metadata.
  5. THE 10 m RULE UNDERSTATES. trunc keeps 0-9 m less than measured at
     every changed domain, always toward less change. The residual is a
     column of the footprint table.
  6. N IS NOT A RATE. Row 0 is a 1996 feature only where ALACE reached
     the dune and 2009 elsewhere.
