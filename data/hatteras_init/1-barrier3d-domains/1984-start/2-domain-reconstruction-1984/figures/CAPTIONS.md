# Figure captions

Written by `HAT_footprint_1984.py` (`write_captions`) and by hand for the figures of the other two scripts. Each heading names the folder under `figures/`: island-wide figures sit in a section's `island/` or placement folder (`seaward/`, `behind-road/`), figures of one example domain in its `rows-added/` or `rows-removed/`; README.md maps them (2026-09-08). The figures carry no in-image titles or footnotes on purpose; use these under them.

## `HAT_row_insert_grid.png` (3-placement/seaward)

(a–c) The 90 Barrier3D domains as the model would hold them, 50 alongshore cells each, every cross-shore row down the page from the two dune rows (right axis in metres; interiors run to 189 rows, white is off the array, cells are not square). Existing cells are shaded by elevation class (m above MHW), so the dune ridge, the backbarrier flat and the sound-side marsh are distinguishable. Where the 1984 dune line lay seaward of the 1997 line, N blank rows (red) are added between the dune and the existing interior, which is pushed down the page; where it lay landward, the existing rows 0 to |N|−1 are hatched (blue) and the interior the model would hold starts below them; the signed count is printed above each changed domain. The dark bar is NC-12 at its measured 1984 position, 20 m wide; it moves down with the interior where rows are added and stays put where rows are removed. Communities and villages along the bottom of each panel, from the site configuration. 29 domains gain 73 rows, 23 lose 47, 38 are unchanged.

## `HAT_row_insert_grid_behindroad.png` (3-placement/behind-road)

(a–c) As `HAT_row_insert_grid.png`, with the same rows placed directly BEHIND THE ROADWAY ROWS instead of at the seaward edge. The roadway in the model is two straight rows at one setback per domain, so the block goes in at int(setback / 10) + 2 rows from interior row 0 with the road at its 1984 setback (2026-09-08), and the strip from the dune crest through the road is kept exactly as measured; the added rows (red, blank) push only the backbarrier down the page. Removals (blue hatching) come out of the interior IN FRONT of the road (2026-09-08): the |N| rows directly seaward of today's roadway rows, so the road's own cells and everything behind them are kept and the crest-to-road strip shortens at its road end; the dark bar there is NC-12 where it is today, and once the rows are gone the model's road at the 1984 setback lands on the old pavement's first row or the row seaward of it. Two road symbols, drawn the same way at every road domain: the filled dark bar is NC-12 as the model holds it in v3, two 10 m rows at the 1984 setback (at GIS 85 rows 4–5, the block starting at row 6); the outlined bar is NC-12 as surveyed, the pavement rows of the 1984 alignment on this surface, i.e. today's setback. Where rows are added the two sit N rows apart, the block between them; where rows are removed they nearly coincide, because the road keeps its cells and the rows in front of it go. Row 0 and the dune do not move. Domains without a model road (GIS 1–5, 8) take the crest row as the anchor instead: the block goes in one row behind it. The insert row per domain is `insert_row_behind_road` in `footprint_1984_by_domain.csv`. N is identical to the seaward placement: 29 domains gain 73 rows, 23 lose 47, 38 are unchanged.

## `HAT_imagery_review_GIS<N>.png` (3-placement/imagery-review/rows-added, rows-removed, unchanged)

The 1984 footprint against the aerial photographs, one figure per reviewed domain (52 changed, 10 unchanged neighbours as controls). Panels (a) and (b) are the same window of the domain in the 1984-09-19, 1997-10-12 photographs of the USGS Henderson release (georeferenced to the 2007 orthophotos, stated accuracy 1.2 m, drawn at 0.5 m with a 2–98 % stretch), (c) the 1 m lidar the model is built on (grey relief, a 1996 foredune on a 2009 backdune). On all three: the 1984 (red) and 1997 (blue) dune lines as digitized, NC-12 in 1984 (dashed) and 2004 (solid), interior row 0 on every profile (thin dashed), the road rows as measured on the 1996 surface (dotted outline) and as placed in v3 under the 1984 setback (dark fill), and both placements of the rows the footprint changes: v3's, behind NC-12 where rows are added or directly in front of it where rows are removed (solid outline, red added / blue removed), and the seaward alternative, the same rows at the dune (purple dashed outline). Domains without a model road (GIS 1–5, 8) anchor v3's rows on the crest row. (d) The photograph's brightness along the domain's 50 profiles: the mean of each 10 × 10 m cell from 250 m seaward of row 0 to the landward edge of the window, the median over profiles (line) and the 25–75 % band, one curve per year, each scaled to its own 2–98 % range so that films of different exposure share an axis; bright is sand, dark is vegetation or water, and a step down going landward is the vegetation line. The vertical marks and shading repeat the lines and rows of the panels above at the median profile. Nothing is picked from the curve; it is a reading aid for the verdict columns of `imagery_review_1984.csv`. Window from the beach to about 150 m behind the landward-most row drawn; scale bar 100 m = 10 cells; ocean to the right.
