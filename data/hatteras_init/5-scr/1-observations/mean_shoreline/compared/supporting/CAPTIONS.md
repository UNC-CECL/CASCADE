# Captions — compared

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`mean_shoreline_compared_1996_2009_2025.png`.** The three CoastSat mean shorelines the hindcast starts from and is graded against, as the island-offset build sees them, in six consecutive sections of Hatteras in one row, south to north from left to right, alongshore up the page, south at the bottom. Each line is the distance from the shared offshore datum along the 100 m model transects, averaged per 500 m domain, with the ocean on the right, computed with the offset build's own intersection (duneline_to_raw_offsets.intersect). 1996 (red, solid) and 2009 (blue, dashed) are means over ±1 yr of the middle of the lidar flights of the start DEMs (1996 NOAA/NASA ALACE, 1995-10-12 to 1997-10-12; 2009 USACE NCMP, 2008-08-17 to 2010-08-17). 2025 (amber, dotted) has no DEM: it is the mean over ±6 months of 2025-08-17, 16 yr after the 2009 centre, and CoastSat ends 2026-01-13, so it spans about 11 months; median satellite positions per transect 20, 28 and 35. The text in each panel gives the range of change per domain over the calibration (1996→2009) and test (2009→2025) periods, seaward +; island medians -4.8 m and +7.7 m. No smoothing. Village spans shaded. Per-domain numbers in mean_shoreline_compared_1996_2009_2025.csv.
