Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`mean_shoreline_compared_1996_2009_2025.png`.** The three CoastSat mean shorelines the hindcast starts from and is graded against, as the island-offset build sees them, in six consecutive sections of Hatteras in one row, south to north from left to right, alongshore up the page, south at the bottom. Each line is the distance from the shared offshore datum along the 100 m model transects, averaged per 500 m domain, with the ocean on the right, computed with the offset build's own intersection (step1_line_to_raw_offsets.intersect). 1996 (red, solid), 2009 (amber, dashed) and 2025 (blue, dotted) are CoastSat means over calendar years (1996-01-01 to 1996-12-31, 2009-01-01 to 2009-12-31, 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31): the advisor's 2026-10-12 setup, in which the 1996 DEM (NOAA/NASA ALACE) and the 2009 DEM (USACE NCMP) start runs whose storms begin the year after; median satellite positions per transect 13, 9 and 37. The text in each panel gives the range of change per domain over the calibration (1996→2009) and test (2009→2025) periods, seaward +; island medians -2.5 m and +4.0 m. No smoothing. Village spans shaded. Per-domain numbers in mean_shoreline_compared_1996_2009_2025.csv.
