# Captions — 1996_2024

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`lrr_1996_2024_halves.png`.** Observed shoreline change rate by GIS domain (1 at Cape Point, 90 at Pea Island). (a) 1996–2024: the mean linear regression rate of the CoastSat transects inside each 500 m domain (domain_lrr_summary.csv), blue and filled where the shoreline moved seaward, red where it moved landward. (b) The same mean over the two model periods, 1996–2010 (grey) and 2010–2024 (black). The spread across transects is in the domain tables, not drawn. Black bars above (a) mark the beach fills placed inside the window, at the footprint the hindcast uses (2014 at GIS 84–89; 2022 at GIS 6–15; 2022 at GIS 21–28); the rates there include the placed sand, and a fill is a step that a single slope fits poorly. The hatched amber boxes in both panels mark the offshore shoals (Avon Shoals GIS 24–39; Wimble Shoals GIS 60–74). Village spans are shaded; the solid hairline is the Buxton groin and the dotted hairlines are the Avon and Rodanthe piers. Both panels share a y axis of ±7 m/yr, the smallest whole metre that holds every value; the single-window figures use ±8, so this figure is not read panel-for-panel against them. The 1996–2024 rate is context: no model run is graded against it.
