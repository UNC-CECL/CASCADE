# Captions — 1996_2024

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`duneline_change_1997_2023_halves.png`.** Net change in dune-line position by GIS domain (1 at Cape Point, 90 at Pea Island). (a) 1997–2023: the 2023 digitized dune line minus the 1997 line, each measured along the 100 m transects from a fixed offshore datum and averaged over the transects of each 500 m domain; blue and filled where the dune moved seaward, red where it moved landward. (b) The same over 1997–2009 (grey) and 2009–2023 (black); the two add up to (a) exactly, since they share the 2009 line. The lines stand in for the model years 1996, 2010 and 2024 (no island-wide imagery at those dates). This is net displacement, not a rate: no survey dates enter it. Black bars above (a) mark the beach fills placed inside the window at the footprint the hindcast uses (2014 at GIS 84–89; 2022 at GIS 6–15; 2022 at GIS 21–28); hatched amber boxes mark the offshore shoals (Avon Shoals GIS 24–39; Wimble Shoals GIS 60–74). Village spans are shaded; the solid hairline is the Buxton groin and the dotted hairlines are the Avon and Rodanthe piers. Both panels share a y axis of ±100 m, the smallest multiple of 10 m that holds every value.
