# Two retirements, filed together

**Dated 2026-08-10**, from git: the last change to either before the 2026-09
reorganisation, which is the best available evidence of when work on them
stopped. It is not necessarily the day a decision was taken.

They share a folder because they share that date, and the convention names a
retirement folder for its date alone. Both were retired for the same reason —
the rate work moved on — so the merge loses nothing, but it is a merge and is
recorded as one rather than left to look like a single set.

retirement is a dated folder with a note, and deletes nothing.
