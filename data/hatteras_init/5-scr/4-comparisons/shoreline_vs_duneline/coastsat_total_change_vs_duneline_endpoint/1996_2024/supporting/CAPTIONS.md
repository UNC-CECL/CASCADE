# Captions — 1996_2024

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`coastsat_total_change_vs_duneline_1996_2024_two_panel.png`.** Trend-implied shoreline change against measured dune-line change by GIS domain (1 at Cape Point, 90 at Pea Island), 1996–2024. (a) The shoreline as the coloured line and fill (blue seaward, red landward) with its single transects as dots; the dune line as the black line. (b) Beach-width change, the shoreline change minus the dune-line change: solid grey where the beach widened, hatched where it narrowed. The shoreline change is the CoastSat trend's: for each transect the 1996–2024 linear regression rate (the ordinary-least-squares slope through every satellite position from 1 January 1996 to 31 December 2024) multiplied by 28 yr, the calendar interval, averaged over the ~10 transects in each 500 m domain. The rate is fitted and evaluated inside the same window, so nothing is extrapolated. The dune-line change is measured: the 2023 digitized dune line minus the 1997 line along the 100 m transects, averaged over the ~5 in each domain, spanning 1997-10-12 to 2023-07-01 (assumed), 25.72 yr. Both are seaward positive, in metres. Beach-width change is shoreline change minus dune-line change: positive where the beach widened, negative where it narrowed; because the two spans differ by +2.3 yr, it also holds that much shoreline drift (over the dune line's own 25.72 yr the mean would be +18.7 m rather than +19.1 m). Black bars above the panel mark the beach fills placed inside the window, at the footprint the hindcast uses (2014 at GIS 84–89; 2022 at GIS 6–15; 2022 at GIS 21–28). Hatched amber boxes mark the offshore shoals (Avon Shoals GIS 24–39; Wimble Shoals GIS 60–74). Village spans are shaded; the solid hairline is the Buxton groin and the dotted hairlines are the Avon and Rodanthe piers. Over the 90 domains the shoreline changed +4.3 m on average and the dune line -14.8 m; the beach widened by +19.1 m (range -31.9 to +107.6 m) and narrowed in 20 of them. r(shoreline, dune line) = 0.77. The y axis is ±100 m on every panel. Beyond ±100 m, off the axis and marked with a triangle at the edge: the beach-width change +108 m at GIS 2.
