Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`coastsat_total_change_vs_duneline_change_between_periods.png`.** How much each side changed between the two periods, by GIS domain (1 at Cape Point, 90 at Pea Island): the second half's net change minus the first half's, seaward positive. Above zero the feature did better in 2010–2024 than in 1996–2010 (it retreated less, or advanced more); below zero it did worse. Blue: the CoastSat trend, each half's linear regression rate times its 14 calendar years. Red: the dune line, each half measured between its own two digitized lines. The grey fill is the gap between them, where the two features changed pace by different amounts; it is not a beach width, since both lines are differences, so it carries no widened/narrowed hatching. Over 90 domains r = 0.40 in metres and 0.41 as rates (m/yr, which removes the dune line's unequal spans of 11.63 and 14.09 yr); the two moved the same way in 60 of 90 domains. Island means: shoreline +20.6 m, dune line +17.5 m. Village spans are the grey strip along the top; the solid hairline is the Buxton groin and the dotted hairlines the Avon and Rodanthe piers; hatched amber boxes mark the offshore shoals. y axis ±100 m.
