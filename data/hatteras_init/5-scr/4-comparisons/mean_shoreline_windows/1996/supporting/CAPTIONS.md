# Captions — 1996

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`mean_shoreline_windows_1996.png`.** The 1996 period's CoastSat mean shoreline over two windows: the calendar 1995–1997 (the window the shoreline island offset v1 was built from) and 1995-10-12 to 1997-10-12, ±1 yr of the 1996 fall East Coast NOAA/NASA ALACE lidar flights (flown 1996-10-09 to 1996-10-16), the start DEM's lidar. (a) The 2-yr mean minus the 3-yr mean along each CoastSat transect, positive where the DEM-centred line is seaward: transects as light dots, the domain mean as the line; the grey band is ±1 Barrier3D cell (10 m). Island mean +0.6 m; after removing it, the domain means vary by sd 2.7 m, the part an offset zeroed on its own minimum can see. (b) The median standard error of the transect means in each domain, for each window. The windows share satellite passes, so the two means are not independent and no significance is attached to (a). Village spans along the top of (a).

**`mean_shoreline_windows_1996_lines_largest.png`.** The 1996 period's CoastSat mean shoreline over two windows, drawn on the USGS aerial photographs of 1996-10-14 (doi 10.5066/P1CXBCDW, stated accuracy 1.2 m): red, the calendar 1995–1997 mean (the shoreline island offset v1's line); blue, the 1995-10-12 to 1997-10-12 mean, ±1 yr of the 1996 fall East Coast NOAA/NASA ALACE lidar (v2's line). The 6 domains whose difference is largest once the island mean (+0.6 m) is removed, south to north. Each panel is the middle 250 m of one Barrier3D domain, north up, all at one scale; the panel width follows the lines. Dots are the per-transect means (~50 m apart) that the lines join. Under each letter, the whole domain's mean difference, 2-yr minus 3-yr, positive seaward. Photograph frames of different exposure meet at straight seams. White is outside the photographs.

The centre domains of the six sites of the mean_shoreline imagery figures, south to north. **`mean_shoreline_windows_1996_datum.png`.** The 1996 period's CoastSat mean shoreline over two windows, as the island-offset build sees it: the calendar 1995–1997 mean (solid grey, the line behind the shoreline offset v1) and the 1995-10-12 to 1997-10-12 mean, ±1 yr of the 1996 fall East Coast NOAA/NASA ALACE lidar (dashed purple, the line behind v2), in six consecutive sections of Hatteras on a 2 x 3 grid, alongshore up the page, south at the bottom. In each wide panel both are the distance from the shared offshore datum along the 100 m model transects, averaged per 500 m domain, with the ocean on the right; they are computed with the offset build's own intersection (duneline_to_raw_offsets.intersect), so the two go through identical code. At this scale the two profiles print as one line, so the narrow strip beside each panel draws the difference itself on the same domain axis: 2-yr minus 3-yr, blue to the right where the 2-yr line is seaward, red to the left where it is landward, the dotted lines ±1 Barrier3D cell (10 m); every strip shares one scale. Island mean +0.6 m; domains beyond one cell: 0 of 90. Village spans shaded. Per-domain numbers in datum_stations_1996.csv.

**`mean_shoreline_windows_1996_island.png`.** Where the 1996 period's CoastSat mean shoreline moves when the window changes from the calendar 1995–1997 to 1995-10-12 – 1997-10-12 (±1 yr of the 1996 fall East Coast NOAA/NASA ALACE lidar), along the whole island in six north-up segments of 15 Barrier3D domains, at one common scale: (a) GIS 1–15 from Cape Point, through (f) GIS 76–90 at the north end. The line is the 3-yr mean shoreline as it lies on the ground, coloured between each pair of CoastSat transects (~50 m) by the 2-yr minus 3-yr difference: blue where the DEM-centred line is seaward, red where it is landward, white where they agree. At this scale 10 m is a fraction of the line's width (edged in grey so it stays visible where the difference is near zero), so the two lines are not drawn separately; mean_shoreline_windows_1996_lines_*.png show them apart. Island outline pale grey, the 500 m model domains as thin boxes, every fifth numbered on the ocean side. Island mean +0.6 m.
