# Captions — 2004_2024

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`lrr_2004_2024.png`.** Observed shoreline change rate by GIS domain (1 at Cape Point, 90 at Pea Island), 2004–2024: for each CoastSat transect the ordinary-least-squares slope of shoreline position against date over the calendar window (1 January 2004 to 31 December 2024, at least 3 positions), seaward positive. The coloured line and fill are the mean of the ~10 transects in each 500 m domain, blue where the shoreline moved seaward and red where it moved landward; the dotted lines are ±1 standard deviation across them; the dots are the single transects, blue or red by their own sign. The dark blue curve is the target a model run is scored against: the same transect rates smoothed alongshore by a LOESS 5 km wide, reverting to the raw domain means over GIS 1–10 (the Oregon Inlet boundary treatment). Where it departs from the coloured line, the smoothing is what moved it. Black bars above the panel mark the beach fills placed inside the window, at the footprint the hindcast uses (2014 at GIS 84–89; 2022 at GIS 6–15; 2022 at GIS 21–28). Hatched amber boxes mark the offshore shoals (Avon Shoals GIS 24–39; Wimble Shoals GIS 60–74). Village spans are shaded; the solid hairline is the Buxton groin and the dotted hairlines are the Avon and Rodanthe piers. The y axis is ±8 m/yr, the bound of every window figure (the largest |domain mean| over all windows plus 1 m, rounded up).
