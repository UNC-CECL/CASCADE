# Captions — chains

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`lrr_chain_1984_2004_2024.png`.** The 1984 → 2004 → 2024 model chain, (a) 1984–2004 above (b) 2004–2024, by GIS domain (1 at Cape Point, 90 at Pea Island): the OLS slope of each CoastSat transect's shoreline position against date over the calendar window, averaged per 500 m domain (the line and fill; dotted ±1 standard deviation), with the dark blue curve the target a run is scored against -- the same rates under a 5 km alongshore LOESS, reverting to the raw domain means over GIS 1–10. Seaward positive; blue and filled where the feature moved seaward, red where it moved landward; the dots are the single transects, blue or red by their own sign. Black bars above a panel mark the beach fills placed in that window at the footprint the hindcast uses; hatched amber boxes mark the offshore shoals; village spans are shaded; the solid hairline is the Buxton groin and the dotted hairlines are the Avon and Rodanthe piers. Both panels share a y axis of ±8 m/yr, the bound of every window figure.
