# Captions — 1996_2026

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`lrr_1996_2026.png`.** Observed shoreline change rate by GIS domain (1 at Cape Point, 90 at Pea Island), 1996–2026. The line is the mean linear regression rate of the CoastSat transects inside each 500 m domain (domain_lrr_summary.csv), blue and filled where the shoreline moved seaward, red where it moved landward; the dotted lines are ±1 standard deviation across those transects. Village spans are shaded; the solid hairline is the Buxton groin and the dotted hairlines are the Avon and Rodanthe piers. The y axis is held at ±12 m/yr on every panel, the largest |mean| over 1996–2015, 2010–2026, 1996–2026, 2010–2020 plus 1 m rounded up, so the panels are directly comparable.

**`lrr_1996_2015_and_2010_2026_over_1996_2026.png`.** Observed shoreline change rate by GIS domain (1 at Cape Point, 90 at Pea Island) for the candidate windows 1996–2015 (black) and 2010–2026 (purple), over the 1996–2026 rate (grey, filled). Each line is the mean linear regression rate of the CoastSat transects inside each 500 m domain (domain_lrr_summary.csv), fitted over the calendar window (1 January of the first year to 31 December of the last), seaward positive; the spread across transects is in the domain tables, not drawn. The record ends 2026-01-13, so a window to 2026 holds only that much of 2026. Black bars above the frame mark the beach fills placed inside 1996–2026 at the footprint the hindcast uses (2014 at GIS 84–89; 2022 at GIS 6–15; 2022 at GIS 21–28); the rates there include the placed sand. The hatched amber boxes mark the offshore shoals (Avon Shoals GIS 24–39; Wimble Shoals GIS 60–74). Village spans are shaded; the solid hairline is the Buxton groin and the dotted hairlines are the Avon and Rodanthe piers. The y axis is ±12 m/yr, the largest |mean| over 1996–2015, 2010–2026, 1996–2026, 2010–2020 plus 1 m rounded up, shared by every figure of the candidate windows.
