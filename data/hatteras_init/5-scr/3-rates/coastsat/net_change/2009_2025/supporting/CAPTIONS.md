# Captions — 2009_2025

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`coastsat_net_change_2009_2025.png`.** Net change in shoreline position per GIS domain: for each transect, the mean CoastSat shoreline in a window around the end date minus the mean in a window around the start date, then averaged over the domain's transects. Start windows are the DEM flight midpoint ±6 months (the island offset's window); end windows are the last modelled year ±6 months (1 Jul to 30 Jun). The target the model's end-minus-start shoreline is scored against. Test period, 2009-2025: the mean CoastSat shoreline over 2024-07-01 to 2026-06-30 (±1 yr of 2025-07-01) minus the mean over 2009-02-17 to 2010-02-17 (±6 mo of 2009-08-17), 15.87 yr apart; the model runs 17 years, 1 Jan 2009 to 1 Jan 2026. CoastSat stops on 2026-01-13, so the end mean actually spans 2024-07-01 to 2026-01-13. Pale line: the mean over each domain's transects (transects that pass the 10-position minimum in both windows). Dark line: the same at 7-domain LOWESS, GIS 1-10 raw, which is how the model side is smoothed. The legend title names the two means differenced. Seaward positive. Beyond ±100 m, off the axis and marked with a triangle at the edge: domain means +149 m at GIS 1; LOWESS +149 m at GIS 1.
