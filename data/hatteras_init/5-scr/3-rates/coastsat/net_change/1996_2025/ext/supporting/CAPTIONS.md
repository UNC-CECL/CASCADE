**`coastsat_net_change_1996_2025_gis1-120.png`.** Net change in shoreline position per GIS domain: for each transect, the mean CoastSat shoreline in a window around the end date minus the mean in a window around the start date, then averaged over the domain's transects. Windows are ±1 yr of the DEM flight dates, except the 2025 end window (±6 mo). Full period, 1996-2025: the mean CoastSat shoreline over 2025-02-17 to 2026-02-17 (±6 mo of 2025-08-17) minus the mean over 1995-10-12 to 1997-10-12 (±1 yr of 1996-10-12), 28.85 yr apart; the full-window run runs 30 years, 1 Jan 1996 to 1 Jan 2026. CoastSat stops on 2026-01-13, so the end mean actually spans 2025-02-17 to 2026-01-13. GIS 1-90 are the model domains; GIS 91-120 (shaded, right of the dashed line) extend the same 500 m domain frame north across Pea Island to Oregon Inlet and are not modelled. Context, not a target. Domain means average +4.8 m over GIS 1-90 and -16.3 m over GIS 91-120. Pale line: the mean over each domain's transects (transects that pass the 10-position minimum in both windows). Dark line: the same at 7-domain LOWESS across all 120 domains, GIS 1-10 raw. At GIS 120, the last domain before Oregon Inlet (5 transects), the LOWESS end (+41.5 m) follows GIS 119 (+69.5 m), not its own mean (-6.9 m). The legend title names the two means differenced. Seaward positive. Beyond ±100 m, off the axis and marked with a triangle at the edge: domain means +169 m at GIS 1; LOWESS +169 m at GIS 1.
