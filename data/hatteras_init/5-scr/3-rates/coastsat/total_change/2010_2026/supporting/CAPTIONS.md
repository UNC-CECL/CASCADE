# Captions — 2010_2026

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`total_change_2010_2026.png`.** Total shoreline change by GIS domain (1 at Cape Point, 90 at Pea Island), 2010–2026: for each CoastSat transect the 2010–2026 linear regression rate (the ordinary-least-squares slope through every satellite position from 1 January 2010 to 31 December 2026) multiplied by 16 yr, the distance the shoreline would have moved at that rate. The rate is fitted on 2010–2026 and evaluated over the SAME window, so nothing is extrapolated — this is total change, not a projection. The coloured line and fill are the mean of the ~10 transects in each 500 m domain, blue seaward and red landward, so the alongshore pattern is the 2010–2026 rate's; the dots are the single transects, blue or red by their own sign (12 beyond the axis, drawn as open circles at its edge). The PURPLE line is the OBSERVED ENDPOINT change over the same 16 yr: per transect the mean position over all of 2026 minus the mean over all of 2010, averaged per domain — no rate anywhere in it. Where it lies above the fill the shoreline ended more seaward than the trend implies; below, more landward. Over the 90 domains with both, observed minus total averages -0.3 m (range -36.6 to +33.3 m). Seaward positive, in metres. Black bars above the panel mark the beach fills placed inside the window, at the footprint the hindcast uses (2014 at GIS 84–89; 2017 at GIS 6–15; 2022 at GIS 6–15; 2022 at GIS 21–28). Hatched amber boxes mark the offshore shoals (Avon Shoals GIS 24–39; Wimble Shoals GIS 60–74). Village spans are shaded; the solid hairline is the Buxton groin and the dotted hairlines are the Avon and Rodanthe piers. The y axis is ±100 m, fixed, the same on every metre figure in 3-rates, 4-comparisons and target_comparison. Beyond ±100 m, off the axis and marked with a triangle at the edge: the total change +160 m at GIS 1; the observed change +180 m at GIS 1; the observed change +108 m at GIS 2.
