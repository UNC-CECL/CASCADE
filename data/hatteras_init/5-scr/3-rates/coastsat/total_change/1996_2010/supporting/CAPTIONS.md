# Captions — 1996_2010

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`total_change_1996_2010.png`.** Total shoreline change by GIS domain (1 at Cape Point, 90 at Pea Island), 1996–2010: for each CoastSat transect the 1996–2010 linear regression rate (the ordinary-least-squares slope through every satellite position from 1 January 1996 to 31 December 2010) multiplied by 14 yr, the distance the shoreline would have moved at that rate. The rate is fitted on 1996–2010 and evaluated over the SAME window, so nothing is extrapolated — this is total change, not a projection. The coloured line and fill are the mean of the ~10 transects in each 500 m domain, blue seaward and red landward, so the alongshore pattern is the 1996–2010 rate's; the dots are the single transects, blue or red by their own sign (3 beyond the axis, drawn as open circles at its edge). The PURPLE line is the OBSERVED ENDPOINT change over the same 14 yr: per transect the mean position over all of 2010 minus the mean over all of 1996, averaged per domain — no rate anywhere in it. Where it lies above the fill the shoreline ended more seaward than the trend implies; below, more landward. Over the 90 domains with both, observed minus total averages -5.6 m (range -43.1 to +16.8 m). Seaward positive, in metres. Hatched amber boxes mark the offshore shoals (Avon Shoals GIS 24–39; Wimble Shoals GIS 60–74). Village spans are shaded; the solid hairline is the Buxton groin and the dotted hairlines are the Avon and Rodanthe piers. The y axis is ±100 m, fixed, the same on every metre figure in 3-rates, 4-comparisons and target_comparison.
