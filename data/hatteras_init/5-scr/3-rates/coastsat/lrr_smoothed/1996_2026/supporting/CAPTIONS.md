# Captions — 1996_2026

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`lrr_lowess5_vs_lowess7_1996_2026.png`.** Observed shoreline change rate by GIS domain (1 at Cape Point, 90 at Pea Island), 1996–2026, smoothed alongshore at two widths. For each CoastSat transect the rate is the ordinary-least-squares slope of shoreline position against date over the calendar window (1 January 1996 to 31 December 2026), seaward positive; the grey dots are those transect rates. The palest line with markers is the mean of the ~10 transects in each 500 m domain, unsmoothed. The two heavier curves are a LOWESS fitted to the transects at 2.5 km (5 domains, frac 0.055) and 3.5 km (7 domains, frac 0.077), the darker being the 7-domain width every run is graded at. Both keep the raw domain means over GIS 1–10, the boundary treatment at Oregon Inlet, so they are identical there by construction. North of GIS 10 the 7-domain curve differs from the 5-domain one by 0.143 m/yr (standard deviation over 80 domains), at most 0.53 m/yr at GIS 29, and by more than 0.5 m/yr at 1 domains. The shaded band on the 7-domain curve is the 95% confidence interval of the transect fits, smoothed the same way (the half-widths are averaged, not combined as independent errors, because neighbouring transects are not independent). Black bars above the panel mark the beach fills placed inside the window, at the footprint the hindcast uses (2014 at GIS 84–89; 2022 at GIS 6–15; 2022 at GIS 21–28). Hatched amber boxes mark the offshore shoals (Avon Shoals GIS 24–39; Wimble Shoals GIS 60–74). Village spans are shaded; the solid hairline is the Buxton groin and the dotted hairlines are the Avon and Rodanthe piers. The y axis is ±12 m/yr, shared by every figure of the candidate windows.
