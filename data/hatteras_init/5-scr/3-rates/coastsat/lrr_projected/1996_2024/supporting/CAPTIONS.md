# Captions — 1996_2024

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`lrr_projected_1996_2024.png`.** Projected net change in shoreline position by GIS domain (1 at Cape Point, 90 at Pea Island), 1996–2024: for each CoastSat transect the 1996–2024 linear regression rate (the ordinary-least-squares slope through every satellite position from 1 January 1996 to 31 December 2024) multiplied by 28 yr, the distance the shoreline would have moved at its long-term rate. The coloured line and fill are the mean of the ~10 transects in each 500 m domain, blue seaward and red landward, so the alongshore pattern is the 1996–2024 rate's; the dots are the single transects, blue or red by their own sign. The black line is the OBSERVED ENDPOINT change over the same 28 yr: per transect the mean position over all of 2024 minus the mean over all of 1996, averaged per domain. Where it lies above the fill the shoreline ended more seaward than its trend predicts; below, more landward. Over the 90 domains with both, observed minus projected averages -2.9 m (range -51.3 to +56.7 m). Seaward positive, in metres. Black bars above the panel mark the beach fills placed inside the window, at the footprint the hindcast uses (2014 at GIS 84–89; 2022 at GIS 6–15; 2022 at GIS 21–28). Hatched amber boxes mark the offshore shoals (Avon Shoals GIS 24–39; Wimble Shoals GIS 60–74). Village spans are shaded; the solid hairline is the Buxton groin and the dotted hairlines are the Avon and Rodanthe piers. The y axis is ±150 m.
