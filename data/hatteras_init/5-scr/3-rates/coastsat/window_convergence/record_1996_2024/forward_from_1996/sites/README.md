# forward_from_1996/sites — eight transects, in full

Eight evenly spaced domains (GIS 1, 13, 26, 38, 51, 64, 77, 90), one transect each: the middle
one of its domain by alongshore order. The readable scale — every position,
every fit, one panel per site. `../all_transects/` runs the same sweep on all
of them and says whether these eight were representative.

```
shoreline_position_window_fits_forward_from_1996.png
                                   the record: positions, the annual median,
                                   and six of the 25 fits. Read first
window_convergence_forward_from_1996.png      the same sweep as an ERROR against the
                                   moving year, on one shared axis
window_convergence_transects.csv   a row per site per window
convergence_summary.csv            a row per site: reference, the marked
                                   window, six convergence entries
supporting/                        the PDFs and CAPTIONS.md
```

Six windows are drawn of the 25 fitted. Drawing them all is a smear in
which the marked window and the reference — the two that matter — are
lost among the rest, each differing from its neighbour by one year of record
(Hannah, 2026-09-23).

## What this run found

- **Headline (CI overlap).** The rate settles after a median of 28 years (range 20–29 across the 8 sites), i.e. the window 1996–2022.
- Against the reference fit's 95% band, 1 of 8 sites (12%) have their 1996–2010 rate already inside the band; the median convergence window is 1996–2023.
- Against ±0.25 m/yr, 1 of 8 sites (12%) have their 1996–2010 rate already inside the band; the median convergence window is 1996–2022.
- Against ±20%, 1 of 8 sites (12%) have their 1996–2010 rate already inside the band; the median convergence window is 1996–2024.
- Against 3× the reference band, 2 of 8 sites (25%) have their 1996–2010 rate already inside the band; the median convergence window is 1996–2021.
- Against ±0.50 m/yr, 1 of 8 sites (12%) have their 1996–2010 rate already inside the band; the median convergence window is 1996–2021.
- Against ±1.00 m/yr, 4 of 8 sites (50%) have their 1996–2010 rate already inside the band; the median convergence window is 1996–2016.
- Against CI overlap, 3 of 8 sites (38%) have their 1996–2010 rate already inside the band; the median convergence window is 1996–2022.
- 7 of 8 sites (88%) enter the band and leave it again before settling, so the FIRST crossing is not the convergence window.
- 2 of 8 sites (25%) change SIGN between the 1996–2010 window and the reference — erosional over one and accretional over the other.
- The largest 1996–2010 error is GIS 13 (usa_NC_0032_0140) at -2.70 m/yr against a reference of +0.46 m/yr.
