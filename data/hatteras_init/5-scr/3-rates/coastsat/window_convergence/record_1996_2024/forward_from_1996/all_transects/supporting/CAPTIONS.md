# Captions — all_transects

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`convergence_alongshore_forward_from_1996.png`.** The same nested sweep run on every CoastSat transect on the island (906 of them), aggregated to the 90 model domains, with the window pinned at 1996 with the end moving out. (a) how many years of record the fitted rate needs before its own 95% interval stops failing to reach the 1996–2024 fit's — CI overlap, the headline tolerance — against the 15 years the 1996–2010 window has (amber). (b) that window's error: its rate minus the 1996–2024 rate, with ±0.25 m/yr as hairlines. (c) the fraction of each domain's transects whose 1996–2010 rate already sits inside each of the three tolerances. Blue is the domain median over its ~10 transects and the grey band their interquartile range, so a domain whose transects disagree cannot pass as one that settled. Across the island the median record length needed is 26 years and 36% of transects have their 1996–2010 rate inside the CI band. Thin grey verticals are the eight sites drawn in the panel figures.

**`tolerance_comparison_forward_from_1996.png`.** What “close enough” costs. Every tolerance the sweep scores, drawn on the same error curve. (a) and (b) are single transects: the black line is the window’s rate minus the 1996–2024 rate, the dashed pairs are the fixed tolerances, and the blue funnel is CI overlap — the only one that is not a band, because it widens with the window’s own uncertainty and narrows onto the reference. The triangles on the zero line mark where each tolerance says the rate has settled. (a) is the transect where the four disagree most, (b) one where they nearly agree. (c) the whole island: the share of the 906 transects settled by a given record length, one curve per tolerance, with the marked window’s 15 years dotted — the horizontal gap between curves is what the choice of tolerance is worth, in years. Island medians: the reference fit's 95% band 1996–2022 (11% of marked windows pass), 3× the reference band 1996–2021 (28% of marked windows pass), ±0.50 m/yr 1996–2020 (31% of marked windows pass), CI overlap 1996–2021 (36% of marked windows pass), ±1.00 m/yr 1996–2014 (52% of marked windows pass). The windows are nested, so every curve reaches 100% at the full record by construction. 3× the reference band and ±0.50 m/yr are nearly the same width on this record — the reference fit’s half-width is about 0.15 m/yr — so their curves in (c) track each other, and the choice between them is not worth arguing about.
