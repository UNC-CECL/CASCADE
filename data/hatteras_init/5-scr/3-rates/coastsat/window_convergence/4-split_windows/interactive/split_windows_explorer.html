The eight transects in the static figures, numbered 1 (Buxton) to 8 (Rodanthe)

Each line is an ordinary least-squares fit to every raw CoastSat position in its window, drawn over the dates that window saw. The cutoff year belongs to both windows (calendar years, inclusive). Rates are in m/yr, seaward positive, with the 95% interval of the slope. A window with fewer than 10 positions gets no fit.

The open circles are the annual median, a guide for the eye only; nothing is fitted to them. Position is CoastSat chainage on an origin that is arbitrary per transect, so compare slopes between transects, not levels.
