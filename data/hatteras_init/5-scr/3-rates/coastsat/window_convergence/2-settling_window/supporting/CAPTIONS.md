# Captions — 2-settling_window

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`years_needed_alongshore.png`.** How many years of CoastSat record each of the island's 906 transects needs before its shoreline change rate (OLS, the target's estimator) comes within a threshold of the 1996–2024 rate and stays there for every longer window. (a) windows starting in 1996, the end moving later; (b) windows ending in 2024, the start moving earlier. The bands stack from the loosest threshold (±1.0 m/yr, darkest, bottom) to the strictest (±0.25 m/yr, lightest, top): the top of each band is the years that threshold needs. 29 years means only the full record matches. The x-axis counts transects south to north, each one unit wide; GIS domain numbers are given beneath it. Village spans are shaded.
