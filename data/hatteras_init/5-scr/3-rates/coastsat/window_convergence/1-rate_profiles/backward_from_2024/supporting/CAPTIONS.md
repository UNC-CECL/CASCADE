# Captions — alongshore_profiles

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`window_profiles_panels_backward_from_2024.png`.** One panel per window: the CoastSat shoreline change rate at all 906 transects (blue, darker for longer windows) against the 1996–2024 rate (purple), on one shared y-axis zoomed to ±5 m/yr: a short window whose rate runs past that is cut at the panel edge. Domains 1 (Cape Point) to 90 (Pea Island). Each panel names its window, its length in years of record and the alongshore Pearson r of its profile against the reference; the last panel is the reference alone, for comparison. The windows are nested, so r rises to 1 at the reference by construction.

**`window_profiles_overlay_backward_from_2024.png`.** The CoastSat shoreline change rate (OLS, the target's estimator) at every one of the island's 906 transects, south to north, fitted over each window of a nested family with the end pinned at 2024: 2023–2024 through 1996–2024. Windows are coloured by length, light (short) to dark (long); the 1996–2024 reference is purple. The y-axis is zoomed to ±10 m/yr, so a short window whose rate runs past that is cut at the edge. The r of each profile against the reference is in ../../2-r_bias_rmse/.
