# Captions — chains

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`duneline_endpoint_chain_1984_2004_2024.png`.** The 1984 → 2004 → 2024 model chain, (a) 1984–2004 above (b) 2004–2023 (for 2004–2024), by GIS domain (1 at Cape Point, 90 at Pea Island): the end digitized dune line minus the start line along the 100 m transects, averaged per 500 m domain (the line and fill), in metres. Seaward positive; blue and filled where the feature moved seaward, red where it moved landward; the dots are the single transects, blue or red by their own sign (1 beyond the axis, drawn as open circles at its edge). Black bars above a panel mark the beach fills placed in that window at the footprint the hindcast uses; hatched amber boxes mark the offshore shoals; village spans are shaded; the solid hairline is the Buxton groin and the dotted hairlines are the Avon and Rodanthe piers. Both panels share a y axis of ±120 m, shared with every endpoint figure. The 2023 dune-line flight date is not known and is assumed to be 1 July.
