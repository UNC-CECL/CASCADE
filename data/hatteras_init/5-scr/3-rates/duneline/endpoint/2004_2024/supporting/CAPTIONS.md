# Captions — 2004_2024

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`duneline_endpoint_2004_2024.png`.** Net change in dune line position by GIS domain (1 at Cape Point, 90 at Pea Island), 2004–2023, the lines standing in for the model years 2004 and 2024: the 2023 digitized dune line minus the 2004 line, each measured along the 100 m transects from a fixed offshore datum (2004-05-25 to 2023-07-01 (the end date assumed; no flight date is known)); the ~5 transects of each 500 m domain are averaged. Seaward positive, in metres; this is net displacement, not a rate. The coloured line and fill are the domain means, blue seaward and red landward; the grey dots are the single transects. Black bars above the panel mark the beach fills placed inside the window, at the footprint the hindcast uses (2014 at GIS 84–89; 2022 at GIS 6–15; 2022 at GIS 21–28). Hatched amber boxes mark the offshore shoals (Avon Shoals GIS 24–39; Wimble Shoals GIS 60–74). Village spans are shaded; the solid hairline is the Buxton groin and the dotted hairlines are the Avon and Rodanthe piers. The y axis is ±120 m, shared by every CoastSat and dune-line endpoint figure so the two can be read against each other.
