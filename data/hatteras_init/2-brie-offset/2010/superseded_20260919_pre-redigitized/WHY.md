# Builds from the earlier digitization — retired 2026-09-19

The `2010` offset builds made from the **pre-re-digitization** dune line,
kept under their old version numbers.

The 2009 line was re-digitized on 2026-09-18. Because that is new source
data rather than a fix to the old build, the numbering restarted (Hannah,
2026-09-19): the current `v1` is the first build from the re-digitized line,
and everything built from the earlier digitization moved here keeping the
numbers it had. The parent `PROVENANCE.md` carries the old-name table.

That is the project's rule for versions — new source data restarts the count,
a same-source fix continues it — so an old `v1` in here and the current `v1`
outside are **different builds from different lines**, not two attempts at the
same thing. That is the trap this note exists to flag.

Nothing here is maintained. Rule 4 of `ORGANIZATION.md`: retirement is a dated
folder with a note, and deletes nothing. Note added 2026-09-22; the folder and
its date are unchanged.
