# Captions — domains

Written by the figure scripts through `hat_figure_style.caption()`; the images carry no titles or footnotes, this file does.

**`offset_1996_duneline_vs_shoreline_domains_GIS01-15.png`.** GIS 1–15 of the 1996 start island as the model builds it from each offset source, one of 6 figures covering GIS 1–90. Every Barrier3D domain of the 1996 start topography (1984-start, dune-topo v2) is drawn at its island offset: (a) with the dune-line build (duneline/v1), (b) with the shoreline build (shoreline/v2), on the same axes. The grids are identical in both panels; only where each domain sits cross-shore changes. Each column of cells is one 500 m domain: its seaward edge is the BRIE shoreline, then the model's 30 m initial beach, the two dune rows and the interior (water masked); landward is up. The dash across each domain in (a) and (b) is where its shoreline WOULD be with the other source: white, over the ocean, where the other source puts the domain further seaward; black, over the beach, where it puts it further landward. (c) gives that move in metres per domain, from (a) to (b): blue up is landward, red down is seaward; the shoreline offset places 3 of 15 domains here further seaward. Grey strips along the bottom of (a) and (b) mark the villages. Offsets are in the model frame, each build zeroed on its own most seaward domain (GIS 76 in both), which is what Cascade receives up to one shared constant (checked against a run's year-0 positions to within 1 m). Vertical exaggeration is large.
